What can I spend today?

Left answers one question. Tell it what you have and how long it has to last; it shows today's share every morning, and moves as you spend.

Free on iPhone, with a free 7-day trial of everything. One optional purchase for the whole family, never a subscription.

How it works

  1. Say what you have. An amount and how long it lasts — or your pay, your bills and what you save, if you're paid on a schedule.
  2. See today's number. Spend less today and tomorrow goes up; spend more and it comes down. The last day offers exactly what is left, to the cent.
  3. Ask before you buy. Type a price and see what it does to every day until payday, then set it aside.
  4. Log in seconds. Favourites, the Lock Screen widget, Siri, Control Center or your Apple Watch.

Private by design

  • No bank connection. You type what you spend.
  • No account and no sign-in.
  • No server, no ads, no analytics, no tracking. The developer collects no data.
  • An encrypted backup you export and keep yourself.

Questions

Is it free?
A new install has 7 days of everything. After that, today's number, logging and your current budget stay free for good. One purchase adds the paycheck plan, goals, “Can I afford it?”, widgets and Apple Watch, insights, full history and export — and it can be shared with your family through Family Sharing.
Does it give financial advice?
No. Left divides the money you give it across the days you choose. What you spend it on is up to you.
Which languages?
English, Turkish, German, French, Spanish and Italian.
