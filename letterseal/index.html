A letter to your future self, sealed until the day it opens.

Write to the person you'll become. Choose the day it opens, press the wax seal, and let it wait. Until then all you see is the envelope and a countdown.

Free on iPhone. One optional purchase, never a subscription.

How it works

  1. Write. Words on paper that feels like paper, a few photos, and with Unlimited your voice or a short video.
  2. Choose the day. Next month, your next birthday, New Year's Day, ten years from now, or let it be a surprise.
  3. Seal it. Press the wax. The letter stays closed until its day, and the app tells you when it's ready.
  4. Open it. Break the seal and read what you once hoped, feared and promised. Then write back.

Letters for someone else

Write to your child for their eighteenth birthday, to a friend for next summer, to your partner for your anniversary. Letterseal sends the sealed letter as a file; it waits in their Letterseal until the date you chose. Opening a letter is always free.

Private by design

  • No account and no sign-in.
  • No server: letters, photos and recordings stay on your iPhone.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking. The developer collects no data.
  • Face ID lock and a password-protected backup file you keep wherever you like.

Because nothing depends on a server, there is no service that can shut down before your letter's day.

Questions

Is it free?
Yes. Up to five sealed letters at a time, photos, letters for other people and the New Year letter are free. Letterseal Unlimited is one purchase that adds unlimited letters, voice and video, more photos and every paper and wax.
Can I open a letter early?
It stays sealed until its date. If you truly need to, you can open it early on purpose: it is a sealed envelope, not a vault.
What if I get a new iPhone?
Save a password-protected backup in Letterseal's settings and restore it on the new phone. Your purchase restores with your Apple Account.
Which languages?
English, Turkish, German, French, Spanish, Japanese and Portuguese (Brazil).
