Countdown: Warranty & Expiry

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: June 23, 2026

Last Updated: September 8, 2026

Previously published as Tiny Countdown. Same app, same developer, same policy.

Summary

Countdown keeps track of dates that matter — warranties, documents, insurance renewals, vehicle service, subscriptions, trips and events. It has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no server of its own.

The app makes no network requests at all, with a single exception: when you buy the one-time unlock or restore a previous purchase, it talks to Apple's App Store. Nothing else you enter ever leaves your device.

Information We Do Not Collect

The developer does not collect personal information from users. Specifically, none of the following is collected:

  • Name, email address, phone number, or physical address
  • Contact list or address book data
  • Photo library access (see Photos below — the app never requests this permission)
  • Precise or approximate location data
  • Financial account information or payment card information
  • Advertising identifiers or device identifiers used for tracking
  • Analytics, crash telemetry, or behavioural data
  • Any user-created content on developer servers — there are no developer servers

Information Stored Locally on Your Device

The app stores the following on your device only:

  • The items you create — titles, dates, categories, notes, colours and icons
  • Reminder settings for each item
  • Archived items you have chosen to keep
  • Photos you attach to an item (see below)
  • App preferences and display settings

None of this is transmitted to the developer or to any external server.

Photos

You can attach a photo of a receipt or a document to an item. This is handled by the system photo picker, which runs outside the app in a separate process supplied by Apple. Because of this, the app is never granted access to your photo library and never asks for that permission. It receives only the single image you pick.

The image you choose is copied into the app's own private storage on your device. It is marked as excluded from iCloud backup. It is deleted when you remove the photo, delete the item, use "Delete all data", or delete the app. It is never uploaded anywhere.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled locally by iOS on your device. There is no push notification server. The contents of a reminder — including the item's title — never leave your device. Permission is requested only when you set up your first reminder, and the app works without it.

Widgets

Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets read your items through a private shared container on your device (an Apple "App Group") that belongs to this app alone. This container is local storage; it is not a network service and nothing in it is transmitted anywhere.

Export

You can export your items as a CSV file. The file is created on your device when you ask for it. It leaves your device only if you choose to send or save it somewhere yourself, using the standard iOS share sheet.

Payments and Purchases

The app offers a single one-time purchase that unlocks additional features. It is processed by Apple through the App Store using StoreKit. The developer does not receive, process, or store your payment card information. All payment handling is managed entirely by Apple. There is no subscription.

Third-Party Services

The app integrates no third-party analytics services, advertising networks, tracking SDKs, or software libraries of any kind. The only Apple platform features it uses are local notifications, WidgetKit, and StoreKit for in-app purchase. These are device-level or App Store platform features and are not used for tracking or profiling.

Data Sharing

The developer does not sell, trade, rent, or share user data with third parties. There is no developer-side database of user-created content. Your information stays on your device.

Data Deletion

You can delete your data at any time by deleting individual items in the app, by using "Delete all data" in Settings, or by deleting the app from your device. Deleting the app removes all locally stored data associated with it, including any attached photos.

Children's Privacy

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children, or from any users, because it does not collect personal information at all. It is designed to function without any personal data being transmitted to the developer.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This policy may be updated if the app's features change in a way that affects how information is handled. When updated, the revised policy is posted on this page with a new date above.
