My reminder never arrived

Reminders arrive at 9:00 in your local time on the birthday itself. With Premium you can also get a heads-up 1, 3, 7 or 14 days before, and choose the time in Settings.

If nothing arrives, check Settings › Notifications › Birthdays on your iPhone and make sure Allow Notifications is on. If notifications were declined once, iOS will not ask again — it has to be turned back on there. The app's Settings screen shows a notice when notifications are off.

Reminders repeat every year on their own. Opening the app now and then keeps heads-up dates and gift notes in them up to date.

How do I add the widget?

Touch and hold an empty spot on your Home Screen, tap Edit › Add Widget, and search for Birthdays. There are small, medium and large Home Screen widgets and three Lock Screen widgets. Touch and hold a placed widget and choose Edit Widget to show only one circle (Family, Friends, Work) — that option, and the One Birthday widget that counts down to a single person, come with Premium.

I paid but the features are locked

Open Settings inside the app and tap Restore Purchase. Make sure you are signed in to the same Apple Account you used to buy. Premium is a one-time purchase, not a subscription, and it works on every device signed in to that account, including with Family Sharing.

If restoring does not work, email me with the date of purchase and I will help.

What does the free version include?

Up to five people, with a reminder on the day, every list widget, ages and milestones, one-tap wishes, search, backup and export, and Siri.

The one-time Premium unlock adds everyone you care about, heads-up reminders at the time you choose, gift ideas and gift history, circles and widget filters, saved wishes, themes, app icons, celebration styles and the One Birthday widget.

Where is my data? Can I move it to a new iPhone?

On your iPhone, and nowhere else. There is no account and no server, so the developer cannot see or recover your data.

To move to a new iPhone, open Settings › Back up now in the app and save the file somewhere you can reach from the new phone (Files, iCloud Drive, AirDrop or email). On the new iPhone, open the app and choose Restore or import a file. Restoring adds people and never removes anyone. A device backup or Quick Start transfer from your old iPhone also carries the app's data over.

You can also import a CSV file from another app, as long as it has a name column and either a birthday/date column or day and month columns.

Can I see birthdays in my Calendar?

Yes. In Settings › Export › Calendar (.ics), share the file to the Calendar app. Each birthday becomes a yearly all-day event. The app does not need access to your calendar for this.

Someone was born on February 29

In years without a February 29, the app counts their birthday on February 28 — in the list, the widgets and the reminders.

How do I delete everything?

Swipe a person to the left to delete them, or open them and tap Edit › Delete Birthday. Deleting the app removes all of its data from your device.
