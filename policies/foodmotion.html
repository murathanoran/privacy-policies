FoodMotion

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 24, 2026

Last Updated: November 24, 2026 (version 1.0)

The short version

FoodMotion shows the energy in food and drink as time spent moving — walking, running, cycling and other activities. It works fully offline. It has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and it never connects to a server run by the developer. Everything you enter stays on your iPhone.

Need help instead? See the FoodMotion support page.

Information we do not collect

The developer does not collect, receive or store any of the following:

  • Name, email address, phone number or physical address
  • Your body weight or any other health information
  • Contacts, photos, location, or microphone or camera input (FoodMotion uses none of these)
  • Payment card or financial account information
  • Advertising identifiers or device identifiers used for tracking
  • Analytics, crash reports collected by the developer, or usage behaviour

Information stored on your iPhone

The food and activity data ship inside the app. To remember your choices, FoodMotion keeps the following on your device only:

  • Your settings: units, energy unit, whether to include resting energy, whether history is on, and — only if you choose to enter it — your body weight, used to scale the estimates
  • Your favourite foods and activities
  • Foods you add yourself, and meals you build or save
  • Comparison history (on by default; it can be turned off or cleared in Settings). History stores the food or meal and its energy, never your body weight

Your data is kept in one file inside the app's own storage, protected by iOS data protection while your iPhone is locked. None of it is transmitted to the developer or to any third party. Your iPhone's own backups may include it, as they do for any app.

Siri and Shortcuts

You can ask Siri, or use the Shortcuts app, how long a walk one serving of a food is. The request is handled by Apple's own system; FoodMotion answers on your device from its built-in data and sends nothing to the developer.

Sharing a card

When you tap Share, FoodMotion draws a picture of the comparison on your device and hands it to the standard iOS share sheet. The card never includes your body weight. Nothing is uploaded by FoodMotion; where the picture goes is up to you and the app you choose.

Purchases

FoodMotion Complete is a one-time purchase processed by Apple through the App Store. The developer never receives your payment details. FoodMotion asks the App Store whether your Apple Account owns FoodMotion Complete so it can unlock it on your devices; that check happens between your iPhone and Apple.

Permissions

FoodMotion asks for no permissions. It sends no notifications.

Third parties

FoodMotion contains no third-party SDKs, analytics services, advertising networks or tracking code. The developer does not sell, rent, trade or share any user data, because no user data is collected. Food energy values come from USDA FoodData Central and activity values from the 2024 Adult Compendium of Physical Activities; both are built into the app, so using them involves no network request.

Deleting your data

Settings ▸ Delete all my data removes your foods, meals, favourites, history and settings from your iPhone. Settings ▸ Clear history removes only the history. Deleting the app removes everything it stored.

Children

FoodMotion does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children, because it does not collect personal information at all.

Estimates, not medical advice

Every number in FoodMotion is an estimate for comparison, based on published averages. It is not a measurement of your body and not medical, nutritional or fitness advice.

Changes to this policy

If a future version changes how information is handled, this page will be updated and the date above will change.
