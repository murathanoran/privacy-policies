Birthday Widget

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: June 23, 2026

Last Updated: September 23, 2026

Introduction

Birthday Widget is designed to help you track upcoming birthdays and display them as a home screen or lock screen widget. This app is designed to work without accounts, tracking, analytics, advertising, or developer-side storage of user-created content.

Information We Do Not Collect

This app does not collect personal information from users. Specifically, the developer does not collect:

  • Name, email address, phone number, or physical address
  • Your contact list or address book (the app never has access to it)
  • Photos or media from your device, unless you explicitly choose a photo for an optional in-app feature
  • Precise or approximate location data, unless explicitly required by a feature you choose to use
  • Financial account information or payment card information
  • Advertising identifiers or device identifiers used for tracking
  • Analytics or behavioral data
  • User-created content stored on developer servers

Information Stored Locally on Your Device

Birthday Widget may store the following data locally on your device: birthday entries (names, dates and, if you add it, a birth year), the circle you assign each person to, an optional phone number, notes, gift ideas and gift history, saved wish messages, widget preferences, reminder settings, and general app settings. This data is kept in the app's private storage on your device, which the app shares only with its own widget. None of this data is transmitted to the developer or any external server.

App Permissions

Contacts. Birthday Widget never requests access to your Contacts and cannot read your address book. You can enter birthdays by hand, or choose Import from Contacts. That option opens Apple's system contact picker, which runs in a separate, private process: only the people you select, with their name, birthday and optionally a phone number, are handed to the app and saved on your device. The rest of your address book is never visible to the app, and the app never writes to your Contacts.

Notifications. Birthday reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device. Permission is requested the first time you save a birthday, and you can change it at any time in iOS Settings.

If a permission is requested for any other optional feature, it is used solely for that feature and is not used for tracking.

Sending Wishes

When you choose Send wish, the app opens Apple's Messages composer (or the system share sheet) with the text you picked and, if you saved one, that person's phone number filled in. You review the message and decide whether to send it. The app never sends messages itself and never transmits any data off your device.

Payments and Purchases

Purchases, if available within this app, are processed by Apple through the App Store using StoreKit. The developer does not receive, process, or store your payment card information. All payment handling is managed entirely by Apple.

Third-Party Services

This app does not integrate third-party analytics services, advertising networks, or tracking SDKs. If the app uses Apple platform features such as local notifications or in-app purchases through StoreKit, these are device-level or App Store platform features provided by Apple and are not used for tracking or profiling users.

Data Sharing

The developer does not sell, trade, rent, or share user data with third parties. There is no developer-side database of user-created content. Your information stays on your device.

Data Deletion

You can delete your data at any time by removing individual entries inside the app where this option is available, resetting app data through app settings where available, or deleting the app from your device. Deleting the app removes all locally stored data associated with it.

Children's Privacy

This app does not knowingly collect personal information from children or from any users, as it does not collect personal information in general. The app is designed to function without any personal data being transmitted to the developer.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This privacy policy may be updated if the app's features change in a way that affects how information is handled. When updated, the revised policy will be posted on this page with a new effective date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
