Common questions

How do I move to the next episode or page?

Tap Next on the entry (on the home list, on its page, or on a widget with Unlimited). Tapped by mistake? Tap Undo.

How do I set an exact place?

Open the entry and tap the big place, or the slider button next to Next. You can set season, episode, page, chapter, lesson, a timestamp or a checkpoint.

How do I add a widget?

Widgets are part of Where Was I Unlimited. Touch and hold your Home Screen, tap Edit, then Add Widget, and search for Where Was I?. For the Lock Screen, touch and hold it and tap Customize.

I bought Unlimited on another iPhone.

Open Settings in the app and tap Restore Purchase, signed in with the same Apple Account.

Is my list backed up?

Your list is part of your iPhone's own iCloud or computer backup. You can also export a backup file from Settings and restore it later — restoring adds what's missing and never deletes.

My reminders don't arrive.

Check Settings > Notifications > Where Was I? on your iPhone and make sure notifications are allowed.
