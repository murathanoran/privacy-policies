If you are reporting a problem, include your iPhone model, your iOS version and what you were doing when it happened. Please do not send photos containing personal information.

How do I save something in one tap?

Tap Snap. Under the viewfinder, pick when you want the reminder — In 1 hour, Tonight, Tomorrow, Weekend, Next week or Someday — then press the shutter. That's it: the photo is saved with that reminder. Tap Add details if you want to give it a title, a purpose (Buy, Return, Pay, Follow up…) or a note. The app remembers the reminder time you chose last.

Prefer to fill in details every time? Turn on Settings › Ask for details after each Snap.

Do my photos end up in my camera roll?

No. Photos you take in One Photo Later stay inside the app — they never appear in your photo library, so your gallery stays clean. When you finish or delete an item, its photo goes too.

My reminder never arrived

Check Settings › Notifications › One Photo Later on your iPhone and make sure Allow Notifications is on. If notifications were declined once, iOS will not ask again — turn them on there. The app shows a note on the photo's screen when this is the case. Focus modes can also hold notifications back.

A reminder is set only when a photo has a time and "Remind me with this photo" is on. Photos set to Someday have no reminder. "Tomorrow" and "Next week" use the morning time you choose in the app's Settings.

The optional daily summary arrives only on days when something is due.

Can I snooze a reminder?

Yes. Touch and hold the notification and choose Done, In 1 hour, Tomorrow or Weekend. In the app, use Later on the photo or on the Up next card.

How do repeating reminders work? (Premium)

On a photo with a reminder time, set Repeat to every day, weekdays, every week, every month or every year. When you mark it done, it comes back on its next date with the same photo; "Skip this time" moves it on without counting it as done. To end it, choose "Stop repeating & archive".

Keep reminding sends a nudge on the next three mornings if something is still open after its reminder.

Siri, Shortcuts, Action Button and Control Center

Say "Snap for later in One Photo Later" to open the camera, or "What's due in One Photo Later" to hear what's due today.

Action Button: Settings › Action Button › Shortcut › Snap for Later. Control Center or Lock Screen: edit Control Center, tap Add a Control and search for One Photo Later (iOS 18 or later).

Save screenshots automatically: in the Shortcuts app, make a shortcut with "Take Screenshot" followed by "Save Image for Later". You can run it with Back Tap (Settings › Accessibility › Touch › Back Tap).

How do I send a photo from another app?

In Photos, Safari, or after taking a screenshot, tap the Share button and choose One Photo Later. If you don't see it, scroll the row of apps to the end, tap More and turn it on. Pick what it's for and when, then tap Save.

Widgets

Snap for Later (free) opens the camera in one tap from the Home or Lock Screen. Up Next (Premium) shows your next photos with a Done button and what's due today on the Lock Screen. If Up Next shows a lock and you have Premium, open the app once — the widget updates as soon as the app confirms your purchase.

What does the free version include?

Up to 10 active photos at a time, the one-tap camera, reminders that show the photo, snoozing, every purpose, text recognition, search, Review, the Archive, the Share button, Siri and Shortcuts, Control Center, and the Snap widget. Finished photos in the Archive don't count toward the 10.

Premium is a one-time purchase, not a subscription, and adds unlimited active photos, repeating reminders, Keep reminding, the Up Next photo widgets with a Done button, and Lists. It can be shared with Family Sharing.

I paid but Premium is locked

Open the app's Settings and tap Restore Purchases, signed in to the same Apple Account you used to buy. If you bought Premium in an earlier version, you still have it — nothing needs to be bought again. If restoring doesn't work, email me with the date of purchase.

I updated from an earlier version — where are my photos?

All still in the app. Every update keeps your photos, titles, notes, lists and reminders. (From version 1, old categories became purposes: Product → Buy, Book → Read, Place → Visit, Document → Do, Idea or Other → Look again.)

Where is my data?

Only on your iPhone. There is no account and no server, and photos are excluded from iCloud backups. That means photos don't sync between devices and can't be recovered by the developer if you delete the app.

How do I delete everything?

Delete one photo from its screen, clear finished ones with Clear in the Archive, or use Settings › Delete All Data to remove everything. Your Premium purchase is not affected.
