Getting started

What's the difference between a milestone and a chapter?

A milestone is a single day that changed something — graduating, moving, a birth. A chapter is a period of your life, like "University years" or "Living in Berlin". Give a chapter a kind (place, school, work, relationship and more) and Milestones shows exactly how long it lasted and paints it onto your life grid.

I only remember the year. Can I still add it?

Yes. When you pick a date, choose Day, Month or Year — or, if you've added your birthday, how old you were at the time. You can also mark a date as approximate. Month- and year-only milestones don't trigger anniversary reminders.

How do I see my life in weeks?

Open My Life and add your birthday. Every square is one week you've lived. Pinch, or use the Weeks / Months / Years switch, to zoom out, and tap any square to see the chapters and milestones of that time. Your birthday stays on your iPhone.

What is "Time Ahead"?

An optional memento mori view. In Settings › Time Ahead you can choose a life span, and the grid will also show the weeks still ahead. It is off by default and the number is never guessed for you — turn it off again at any time.

Where is On This Day?

Tap the calendar button at the top of the Timeline. It shows what today, this week and this month have meant in earlier years.

Unlock and purchases

What's free and what's in the unlock?

Milestones is free for up to 5 milestones and 1 chapter. A single one-time purchase unlocks unlimited milestones and chapters, the Life Poster, widgets, Life in Numbers, the Life Story Book, multiple photos per milestone and custom categories. There is no subscription.

I bought the unlock on another iPhone.

Open Settings in the app and tap Restore Purchase, signed in with the same Apple Account. If you use Family Sharing or Ask to Buy, the unlock appears as soon as it's approved.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Widgets and keepsakes

How do I add a widget?

Widgets are part of the one-time unlock. Touch and hold your Home Screen, tap Edit, then Add Widget, and search for Milestones. Choose Life in Weeks or On This Day. For the Lock Screen, touch and hold it and tap Customize.

How do I print my Life Poster?

In My Life, tap the share button above the grid. Choose the grid (weeks, months or years), a style and a title, then export a vector PDF (best for printing at any size) or a high-resolution image.

Your data

How do I move my milestones to a new iPhone?

Your archive is part of your iPhone's own iCloud or computer backup. You can also use Settings › Export Backup and later Import Backup on the new iPhone. Merging never deletes anything, and a failed import leaves your existing data untouched.

I updated from Milestones 2 — is everything still there?

Yes. Every milestone, chapter, photo and category carries over. Existing milestones keep their exact dates and existing chapters get the kind "Other", which you can change any time.

My anniversary reminders don't arrive.

Turn on Anniversary Reminders in the app's Settings, then check Settings › Notifications › Milestones on your iPhone and make sure notifications are allowed. Reminders are only scheduled for milestones with an exact day.

Does Milestones upload my data?

No. There is no account, no server, no analytics and no ads. Photos are chosen with Apple's photo picker, so the app never has access to your photo library. Your life story stays on your iPhone.
