Support

How does Last Time know when something is due?

Every time you tap Done, the date is saved. You can set a rhythm for an item, such as every 4 days or every 2 weeks. If you don't, Last Time learns one from your history after you have logged it a few times. The ring around each item fills as the next due day gets closer.

I forgot to tap Done

Open the item and tap Log an earlier time. To fix a time you already logged, tap it in the item's History and change the date, or swipe left on it to remove it. With Premium you can also add a short note to any time.

My reminder never arrived

Reminders are part of Premium. Turn them on in an item's detail screen. They arrive on the day the item is due, at the time chosen in Settings (9:00 by default). In Settings you can also ask to be reminded again if it's still not done, or switch to one daily summary instead of a notification per item.

If nothing arrives, check Settings › Notifications › Last Time on your iPhone and make sure Allow Notifications is on. If notifications were declined once, iOS will not ask again — they have to be turned back on there. Paused items never send reminders.

Siri and Shortcuts

Ask Siri "When did I last water the plants in Last Time?" or "What's due in Last Time?" — use the name of one of your own items. Asking is free. Marking an item done with Siri ("Mark water the plants done in Last Time") is part of Premium.

Siri phrases currently work in English. All three actions are also in the Shortcuts app, where you can use them in your own automations in any language.

Control Center and the Action Button

On iOS 18 or later, open Control Center, tap +, then Add a Control and search for Last Time. Choose which item the control marks done. On iPhones with an Action Button you can pick the same control in Settings › Action Button. This control is part of Premium.

Widgets

Long-press your Home Screen, tap Edit › Add Widget and search for Last Time. For the Lock Screen, long-press it, tap Customize › Lock Screen and add a widget. Widgets show what's due for free; tapping Done right in the widget is part of Premium.

Pausing an item

For seasonal things — the pool, winter tires — open the item and tap Pause, or long-press it in the list. A paused item keeps its history, has no due date and sends no reminders until you resume it.

I paid but the features are locked

Open Settings inside the app and tap Restore Purchase. Make sure you are signed in to the same Apple Account you used to buy. The unlock is a one-time purchase, not a subscription, it supports Family Sharing, and it works on every device signed in to that account.

If restoring does not work, email me with the date of purchase and I will help.

What does the free version include?

Five items, with their full history. Learned rhythms, logging an earlier time, editing past dates, widgets that show what's due, asking Siri, and backups are all free.

The one-time unlock adds unlimited items, rhythm insights and the activity calendar, reminders and the daily summary, tapping Done from widgets, Control Center and Siri, categories, notes, and CSV export.

Where is my data?

On your iPhone, and nowhere else. There is no account and no server. The app makes no network requests except to Apple when you purchase or restore.

Because of this, data is not synced between devices and cannot be recovered by the developer if you delete the app. To keep your own copy, use Back up to a file in Settings and save the file somewhere safe, such as Files or your computer.

Moving to a new iPhone

Before you switch, open Settings › Back up to a file in the app and save the file. On the new iPhone, tap Restore from a backup and pick that file. Restoring adds missing items and history and never deletes what is already there. On the free version, restoring stops at five items.

How do I delete everything?

Swipe an item to delete it, or long-press it and choose Delete. Deleting the app removes all of its data from your device.
