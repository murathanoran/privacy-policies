How do I play?

Drag one straight line across the shape and lift your finger. Split measures the true area on each side and shows the two percentages; the closer to 50.00% / 50.00%, the better. A moment later the dashed line shows where the perfect cut was, at the same angle as yours. The cut counts as if your line ran on for ever in both directions.

What is free, and what is Split Complete?

Free, always: Classic (endless shapes), Run, today's Daily with your streak and its share text, friend challenges, the first two Trials packs plus the first shape of every other pack, the Fractions taster, your Precision rating and your statistics, and the share card.

Split Complete is a single, optional one-time purchase. It adds the other ten Trials packs (120 hand-made shapes), Fractions in full (thirds, quarters, fifths and two fifths, endless), the daily archive and deeper statistics. There is no subscription and there are no ads.

I bought Split before. Do I pay again?

No. Everyone who bought Split when it was a paid app, and everyone who has bought Split Complete, keeps everything it includes now — new packs and modes too. If anything shows as locked, for example on a new iPhone or iPad, open Settings ▸ Restore Purchase while signed in with the Apple Account you bought Split with.

I bought Split Complete and things are still locked

Open Settings ▸ Restore Purchase. It asks the App Store whether your Apple Account owns Split Complete and tells you plainly either way. Make sure you are online and signed in with the same Apple Account. Family Sharing is not available for this purchase.

How do friend challenges work?

Tap Challenge a friend ▸ Start one and play ten shapes. At the end, send the code with your score. Your friend opens Split, taps Enter a code, pastes your message and plays the same ten shapes; Split then shows both scores. No account and no internet connection are needed. A run played from someone else's code does not count towards your own records, because its shapes can be replayed.

How does the Daily streak work?

Every day you play the Daily set extends your streak; missing a day starts it over. Days you play later from the archive never change your streak. Split sends no reminders. The Daily number in a shared result counts from the first day Daily existed.

What is the Precision rating?

One number from 0 to 100 describing how good your eye is right now. It comes from the median of your recent first attempts, on a scale where 50 means typically half a percent off and 85 means a tenth of a percent. Retries, Fractions and challenges you accepted do not count towards it. Nothing is unlocked by it; it is only a description.

I can't draw a straight line with my finger

Turn on Settings ▸ Precise Cut Controls. Each cut is then placed with an angle control and a position control and made with the Cut button. It works with VoiceOver and Switch Control. Split also follows your device's text size, Reduce Motion and dark mode.

Does Split work on iPad?

Yes. From version 3.0 Split runs natively on iPad, in any orientation. Your purchase works on every device signed in with the same Apple Account.

My result feels wrong

Split measures exact geometric area, not what looks balanced — that is the whole game. Shapes with holes, notches, long arms or thin ends put the half somewhere the eye does not expect. The dashed line after each cut shows the exact answer at your angle.
