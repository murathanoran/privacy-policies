Contact

Email murathanoran1@gmail.com. One person reads every message, usually within two business days. Your iPhone model and iOS version help. Please never send the contents of a letter or a backup password — they are not needed and nobody should ever ask you for them.

The app used to be called Future Me

Same app, new name. Updating keeps every letter you sealed, its photos and its opening date, and a previous purchase of Unlimited carries over automatically.

What is free, and what is Letterseal Unlimited?

Free: up to five sealed letters at a time, two photos per letter, one surprise letter, letters for someone else (words and photos), ivory and night paper with gold or crimson wax, “This year’s letter”, writing questions, replies, the yearly reminder, widgets, the live countdown, Face ID lock, password-protected backups and keepsake PDFs.

Letterseal Unlimited is a single one-time purchase. It adds unlimited sealed letters, video of up to three minutes, voice messages of up to five minutes, up to six photos per letter, surprise dates for every letter, and every paper and wax colour with your initials pressed into the seal. There is no subscription and there are no ads. Letters you already sealed always open, whether or not you buy it.

I bought Unlimited and something is locked

Open Settings ▸ Restore Purchases while online and signed in with the Apple Account you bought it with. The app tells you plainly whether a purchase was found.

When does a letter open?

At 9:00 AM on the day you chose, in your phone's time zone. You get a reminder the day before and another when it opens, as long as notifications are allowed for Letterseal in the Settings app. Changing the phone's clock does not open a sealed letter early.

What is a surprise date?

You pick a window — 1 to 3 months, 3 to 12 months or 1 to 3 years — and Letterseal picks the day in secret. Nothing shows it: not the list, not the widget, not the reminders. You find out when it opens.

How do I send a letter to someone?

Tap New Capsule, choose For someone else, write their name and the letter, pick the day it opens and seal it. Then tap Send to … and hand it over with AirDrop, Messages, Mail or Files. It travels as one “.sealedletter” file; there is no account and no server in between.

The person opens the file with Letterseal (free on the App Store) and the letter stays sealed in their app until the day. The opening date and your names are visible on the outside, like an envelope; the letter itself is encrypted. It is a promise the app keeps rather than a vault, so send it somewhere you trust.

Someone sent me a sealed letter

Tap the “.sealedletter” file in Messages, Mail or Files and choose Letterseal. It appears on your list as “A letter from …” and opens on its day; until then even its title stays sealed. Letters you receive never count toward the free limit. Opening the same file twice adds it only once.

What is “This year’s letter”?

In December and January, Letterseal offers a letter about the year that is ending, with questions to start you off. It opens at 9:00 AM on the next New Year’s Day.

Adding a video

With Unlimited, a letter can carry one video of up to three minutes, chosen with the system photo picker. Letterseal re-encodes it on your phone, removing location and other details, so it fits comfortably in backups and sealed letters.

Can I read a letter early?

Yes, deliberately. On a sealed letter's countdown screen, tap Edit Message and confirm. Most of a letter's value is in not knowing yet, so the app asks first.

Moving to a new iPhone

In Settings ▸ Back Up, choose a password and save the backup file somewhere you trust, such as the Files app or a computer. On the new phone, install Letterseal and use Settings ▸ Restore from Backup with the same password. Restoring only adds letters that are missing; it never changes letters already on the phone.

The password is never stored. If it is lost, the backup cannot be opened by anyone, including the developer.

The widget doesn't appear

Open Letterseal once after installing or updating, then touch and hold the Home Screen or Lock Screen, tap Edit, then Add Widget, and search for Letterseal. To keep letter names off the Lock Screen, turn off Show names outside the app in Settings (it starts off on a new install).

Languages

Letterseal is available in English, Turkish, German, French, Spanish, Japanese and Portuguese (Brazil), and follows your iPhone’s language. To choose one just for Letterseal, open the Settings app ▸ Apps ▸ Letterseal ▸ Language.

Privacy

Letterseal has no account, no server and collects no data; your letters stay on your phone. Read the full privacy policy.
