How do I play?

Watch the point move. When it disappears and the screen reads TRACE, touch the start point and draw the same movement in one stroke — the same shape, at the same speed, stopping where it stopped. Lift your finger to see both lines together. A score of 70 completes a challenge; the word under the score tells you the one thing to fix: TOO FAST, MISSED PAUSE and so on.

What is free, and what is TRACE Complete?

Free, always: the daily workout and your streak, the weekly Memory Check, challenges 1 to 25, one Endless run and one Chain run a day, Movement Memory with 30 days of history, Relaxed Pace and every comfort setting, and sharing.

TRACE Complete is a single, optional one-time purchase. It adds the rest of the 150 challenges, unlimited Endless and Chain runs, your full history and your skill trends. There is no subscription and there are no ads.

If you played TRACE 2.0 and had already gone past challenge 25, you keep challenges 1 to 40 for free, as before.

I bought TRACE 1.0. Do I pay again?

No. Everyone who owned TRACE 1.0 keeps everything, automatically. If anything shows as locked — for example on a new iPhone — open Settings ▸ Restore Purchase, signed in with the Apple Account you bought TRACE with.

I bought TRACE Complete and things are still locked

Open Settings ▸ Restore Purchase. It asks the App Store whether your Apple Account owns TRACE Complete and tells you plainly either way. Make sure you are online and signed in with the same Apple Account. Family Sharing is not available for this purchase.

What is the daily workout?

Three movements, about two minutes: a warm-up, the day's main movement (the same for everyone, as Daily TRACE always was) and a recall movement you hold in mind for a moment before you trace. Your day's score is the average of the three.

How does the streak work?

Every day you finish the workout counts. Missing one day does not break your streak — that day is a rest day. Missing two days in a row starts it over. TRACE sends no reminders.

What is Chain?

Sequence memory, with movement. One path grows one link longer every round, and each round you trace the whole path from the start. The first round you miss ends the run; the number of links in the longest path you traced is your span.

What is the Memory Check?

The same five movements every week, on every device, so you can compare this week with your first check. You can take it once a week, at any time, and your best result of the week counts. It is a score within this game, not a medical or cognitive test.

The movements are too fast for me

Turn on Settings ▸ Relaxed Pace. Every movement is then shown at three-quarter speed, and your timing is judged against what you saw. Bolder Lines makes the point and the lines easier to see. TRACE also follows your iPhone's text size.

Does TRACE work on iPad?

Yes. From version 3.0 TRACE runs natively on iPad, in any orientation, with a larger canvas. Your purchase works on every device signed in with the same Apple Account.

What is Endless?

A run of movements that ask a little more of your memory each time — longer, with changes of pace, pauses and a wait before you trace. Each movement is shown once; the first one that does not pass ends the run. Your best run is kept.

What is Movement Memory?

One number, 0 to 1000: your ten best attempts of the last seven days, each weighted by how much that movement asks you to remember. Practice never lowers it — a weak attempt simply does not count — but days you don't play slowly leave the window. It is a measure of this game only, not a medical or cognitive assessment.

My score feels wrong

TRACE scores the path, the direction, the rhythm and the timing separately. The most common surprise is speed: a perfect shape drawn twice as fast as shown will not score well. Start exactly on the point, and keep your finger down until the end of the movement.
