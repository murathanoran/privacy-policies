Sunpenny — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026 · applies to Sunpenny 4.0 and later · Developer: Murathan Öran

The short version

Sunpenny does not collect your data. The developer has no server, no account system, no analytics and no advertising, and never receives your budget, your expenses or anything about you.

Your data stays on your devices. It leaves them only when you choose: when you turn on iCloud Sync (then it is kept in your own private iCloud, which only your devices can read), or when you make a backup or an export yourself.

What the developer collects

Nothing. The app does not collect, receive, store, sell or share any personal or financial information. There is no sign-up, no email address, no password and no bank connection. You type what you spend; nothing is linked to anything.

The App Store privacy label for Sunpenny is "Data Not Collected".

Where your data is kept

Your budgets, expenses, paycheck plan, set-asides, savings goals, categories and settings are stored in the app's database on your device (Apple's SwiftData), inside the app's own container.

Apple Watch and widgets. So that your widgets, your Lock Screen and the Apple Watch app can show today's number, the app shares the figures they need through an App Group container on your device. The Apple Watch app receives today's figures from your paired iPhone over Apple's encrypted connection between your own devices (WatchConnectivity). None of this passes through a server of the developer's — there isn't one.

Shopping mode (Live Activity). If you pin today's number to the Lock Screen and the Dynamic Island, the Live Activity is created and updated on your iPhone only. No push service is used for it. It ends by itself after at most 8 hours, and never later than midnight. While App Lock is on, the amount is marked as private, so iOS hides it while your iPhone is locked.

iCloud Sync (optional, off by default)

Sunpenny 4.0 can keep your budget the same on your iPhone and iPad through your own iCloud. It is off until you turn it on in Settings › iCloud Sync (a Sunpenny Premium feature).

When it is on:

  • Your budgets, expenses, plan, goals and categories are stored with Apple's CloudKit service in your private iCloud database, and are shared only between your own devices signed in to the same Apple Account.
  • Apple stores and transfers this data for you. The developer cannot see, read or receive your private iCloud database in any way, and has no server of its own. The data is subject to Apple's iCloud terms and Apple's privacy policy.
  • Turning sync off deletes nothing: your device and iCloud each keep what they have.
  • If you sign out of iCloud or switch Apple Account, iOS removes the synced copy from your device; it stays in that iCloud account. While sync is on, the app keeps its own copy of your data in its container on your device each time you leave the app, so it can offer to put it back. These copies stay on your device (like the rest of the app's data, they are part of your device's own iOS backup to iCloud or a computer, if you make one) and are removed by Erase All Data.

Your purchase and free trial, App Lock, reminders and favorites are set up on each device and are not synced.

Backups and exports — you choose where they go

  • Backup: a file you create, encrypted with a passphrase only you know (AES-GCM, key derived with PBKDF2). The developer cannot open it. You decide where it goes (Files, AirDrop, a message…).
  • Automatic backups (optional, off until you turn them on): the same encrypted file, made by the app on your device once a week and when a new budget starts; the newest five are kept. They stay on your device, in the Files app under On My iPhone › Sunpenny › Automatic Backups (On My iPad on an iPad). Their passphrase is kept in this device's Keychain so the app can make them without asking; it is never synced or sent anywhere.
  • CSV export / import: a plain spreadsheet file you create or choose. An imported file is read on your device and stays there.

None of these is ever sent anywhere by the app on its own.

Permissions the app may ask for

  • Face ID / Touch ID / passcode — only if you turn on App Lock. Face ID is handled entirely by iOS; the app never sees your face data, it only learns whether you were recognized.
  • Notifications — only if you turn on reminders (daily reminder, budget ending, payday, trial ending). They are scheduled on your device. While App Lock is on, they don't show amounts.

The app does not access your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos, calendar, health data or advertising identifier.

Purchases

Sunpenny Premium is a one-time, non-consumable in-app purchase. Purchases and restores are handled entirely by Apple's App Store. The developer never sees your payment details. The app checks your purchase with Apple's StoreKit on your device. With Family Sharing, members of your family group can use the purchase too.

The free trial is timed on your device (stored in the device's Keychain, never synced or sent anywhere).

Analytics, tracking and advertising

Sunpenny contains no analytics, no tracking, no advertising, no third-party SDKs and no advertising identifier. The app's own code makes no network requests. The only network traffic is Apple's own: the App Store (StoreKit) for purchases, and CloudKit when you turn on iCloud Sync.

Deleting your data

  • Erase All Data (Settings) removes your budgets, expenses, plan, goals and settings straight away. Only whether iCloud Sync is on stays as it was. It also deletes the automatic backups in Files › On My iPhone › Sunpenny and their passphrase on this device. Backups you saved anywhere else are not touched.
  • With iCloud Sync on, Erase All Data also deletes your data from iCloud and your other devices as soon as the app can reach iCloud.
  • Deleting the app removes your budget data and automatic backups from that device. iOS may keep the app's small Keychain items (the trial note below and, if you used automatic backups, their passphrase); Erase All Data in the app removes the passphrase. Deleting the app does not delete the copy in your iCloud; you can remove it in the iOS Settings app (your name › iCloud › Manage Storage › Sunpenny).
  • What the app keeps on purpose: a small note in the device's Keychain that the free trial (and the one-time free tries of some features) were used, so erasing or reinstalling doesn't restart them. It contains no budget data and is never synced or sent anywhere.

Children's privacy

Sunpenny does not collect information from anyone, including children.
