What is free, and what is One Breath Complete?

Free, always: Calm, Box and 4-7-8 breathing; sessions from one breath up to five minutes; haptic guidance; the dimmed "eyes closed" screen; the Lock Screen Live Activity; widgets, the Control Center control and Siri; one daily reminder; Apple Health; and the last seven days of history.

One Breath Complete is a single, optional one-time purchase. It adds seven more techniques (Physiological Sigh, Coherent, Wind Down, Triangle, Steady, Energize, Deep Box), rhythms you build yourself, the sound guide, sessions of 10 minutes and longer, your full history with "What helps you", and up to six reminders. There is no subscription.

I bought the unlock in an earlier version. Do I pay again?

No. One Breath Complete uses the same purchase as the earlier "Unlock All", so it unlocks automatically. If it doesn't, open Settings ▸ Restore Purchase.

I bought Complete and things are still locked

Open Settings ▸ Restore Purchase. It asks the App Store whether your Apple Account owns One Breath Complete and tells you plainly either way. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple Account you bought it with and that you are online. Family Sharing is not available for this purchase.

I can't feel the haptics

Check Settings ▸ Haptic guidance in One Breath, try the Strong setting, and make sure System Haptics is on in the iPhone's Settings ▸ Sounds & Haptics. Haptics pause while the screen is locked — iOS does not allow apps to vibrate in the background. Keep the screen on (One Breath keeps it awake for you, and the dim mode makes it almost black), or turn on the sound guide.

Can a session keep going when my screen is locked?

Yes, with the sound guide (One Breath Complete) turned on: soft tones keep guiding you and the session continues. Without sound, the session time keeps counting and One Breath picks up at the right moment when you come back.

How do I start a session from the Lock Screen or the Action Button?

On iOS 18 or later, add the Start Breathing control to Control Center or the Lock Screen, or assign it to the Action Button in the iPhone's Settings. You can also say "Start breathing in One Breath" to Siri, or add the One Breath widget.

Mindful Minutes don't appear in Apple Health

Turn on Settings ▸ Save to Apple Health in One Breath and allow it when iOS asks. If you declined, open the Health app ▸ your profile ▸ Apps ▸ One Breath and allow Mindful Minutes. Only finished sessions of at least 30 seconds are saved.
