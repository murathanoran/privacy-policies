What is Calm Now?

A one-minute reset you can start without choosing anything: two deep sighs (a double breath in, then one long breath out), followed by four slow breaths that are longer out than in. It is free on iPhone and Apple Watch, and you can start it from the app, a widget, Control Center, Siri ("Calm now with One Breath") or a watch-face complication.

What is free, and what is One Breath Complete?

Free, always: Calm Now, Calm, Box and 4-7-8 breathing; sessions from one breath up to five minutes; haptic guidance; the dimmed "eyes closed" screen; the Lock Screen Live Activity; widgets, Control Center controls and Siri; one daily reminder; Apple Health; and the last seven days of history. On Apple Watch, Calm Now is free.

One Breath Complete is a single, optional one-time purchase — no subscription. It adds Moments, seven more techniques (Physiological Sigh, Coherent, Release, Triangle, Steady, Energize, Deep Box), rhythms you build yourself, the sound guide, sessions of 10 minutes and longer, your full history and "What helps you", more reminders, and on Apple Watch every technique, your own rhythms and every length. You can try any Complete technique or Moment once for free.

What are Moments?

Short sequences chosen by situation instead of technique: Before a Meeting, After a Hard Call, Racing Thoughts, Midday Reset, Before Focus and Quick Lift. Each takes about one and a half to two minutes and starts right away.

How do I use One Breath on Apple Watch?

Open the Watch app on your iPhone, find One Breath and tap Install, or get it from the App Store on your watch. To start from your watch face, edit the face and add the One Breath complication Calm Now or Breathe (your last technique).

The taps tell you what to do without looking: they get closer together while you breathe in, spread out while you breathe out, and tick once a second while you hold. You can lower your wrist and close your eyes — the session keeps guiding you with the screen off. Choose Minimal, Guided or Strong in One Breath on your iPhone under Settings ▸ Apple Watch. Rhythms you create on iPhone appear on the watch, and sessions from the watch appear in your iPhone history.

I bought the unlock in an earlier version. Do I pay again?

No. One Breath Complete uses the same purchase as the earlier "Unlock All", so it unlocks automatically on iPhone and Apple Watch. If it doesn't, use Restore Purchase (below).

I bought Complete and things are still locked

On iPhone, open Settings ▸ Restore Purchase. On Apple Watch, tap any locked technique and choose Restore Purchase. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple Account you bought it with and that you are online.

I can't feel the haptics

On iPhone, check Settings ▸ Haptic guidance in One Breath, try the Strong setting, and make sure System Haptics is on in the iPhone's Settings ▸ Sounds & Haptics. On Apple Watch, try the Strong wrist-haptic style and check the watch's own Sounds & Haptics settings.

Why did my session pause when I locked my iPhone?

iPhone can't play haptics while the screen is locked, so a session without sound pauses until you come back — time without guidance is never counted. To keep going with the screen locked, turn on the sound guide (One Breath Complete), or breathe with One Breath on Apple Watch. If a phone call or another app interrupts the sound guide, the session also pauses.

How do I start a session from the Lock Screen or the Action Button?

On iOS 18 or later, add the Start Breathing or Calm Now control to Control Center or the Lock Screen, or assign it to the Action Button in the iPhone's Settings. You can also say "Start breathing in One Breath" or "Calm now with One Breath" to Siri, or add the One Breath widget to your Home Screen or Lock Screen.

Mindful Minutes don't appear in Apple Health

Turn on Settings ▸ Save to Apple Health in One Breath and allow it when iOS asks. If you declined, open the Health app ▸ your profile ▸ Apps ▸ One Breath and allow Mindful Minutes. Only finished breathing time of at least 30 seconds is saved; sessions from Apple Watch are saved through your iPhone.
