Perfect Line

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 21, 2026

Last Updated: October 30, 2026 (version 3.0)

Introduction

Perfect Line is a route puzzle game for iPhone and iPad: on each board you find the shortest route from a start dot to a goal ring and draw it in one line, earning up to three stars. The app works without accounts, tracking, analytics, advertising, or developer-side storage of anything you do. Every level, the Daily Line and hints work entirely offline.

Information We Do Not Collect

This app does not collect personal information from users. Specifically, the developer does not collect:

  • Name, email address, phone number, or physical address
  • Contact list or address book data
  • Photos or media from your device
  • Precise or approximate location data
  • Financial account information or payment card information
  • Advertising identifiers or device identifiers used for tracking
  • Analytics or behavioral data
  • User-created content stored on developer servers

Information Stored Locally on Your Device

Perfect Line stores the following on your device only: which levels you have finished, your stars, best scores, best lines and attempt counts; your Daily Line results and streak; whether you have bought Perfect Line Complete (or own it from a paid version); and your settings (appearance, haptics, sound effects, Relaxed Drawing, showing your best line). None of this is transmitted to the developer or to any server. It may be included in your device backups, which are under your control.

App Permissions

Perfect Line requests no permissions: no location, camera, microphone, photos, contacts, notifications or tracking. Sharing a result uses the system share sheet, and only the image you choose to share leaves the app, sent by you to the destination you pick.

Third-Party Services

This app integrates no third-party analytics, advertising networks or tracking SDKs, and no third-party code at all. Gameplay runs entirely on your device. The only network activity is Apple's own In-App Purchase system (StoreKit), used when you view, buy or restore Perfect Line Complete; Apple processes that purchase under its own privacy policy, and the developer receives no personal or payment information from it.

Data Sharing

The developer does not sell, trade, rent, or share user data with third parties. There is no developer-side database of user-created content. Your information stays on your device.

Data Deletion

You can erase your progress at any time with Settings ▸ Reset Progress, or delete the app to remove everything it stored. Purchases are tied to your Apple Account and can be restored at any time.

Children's Privacy

This app does not knowingly collect personal information from children or from any users, as it does not collect personal information in general. The app is designed to function without any personal data being transmitted to the developer.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This privacy policy may be updated if the app's features change in a way that affects how information is handled. When updated, the revised policy will be posted on this page with a new effective date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
