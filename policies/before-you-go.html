Before You Go

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: June 23, 2026

Last Updated: October 2026 (version 3.0)

The Short Version

Before You Go does not collect, upload, or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no servers, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking. Your checklists, your check history and any proof photos you take live on your iPhone and nowhere else. The app never asks for your location.

Information We Do Not Collect

The developer does not collect:

  • Name, email address, phone number, or physical address
  • Contacts, or photos and media from your photo library (the app never asks for photo-library access)
  • Precise or approximate location
  • Financial account or payment card information
  • Advertising identifiers or device identifiers used for tracking
  • Usage analytics, behavioral data, or crash reports sent to third parties
  • User-created content stored on developer servers

Information Stored Locally on Your Device

Before You Go stores the following only on your device:

  • Your checklists, their items, and hotel stays you add
  • Your check history: when you finished each check and the time each item was ticked (added in version 2.0)
  • Proof photos you choose to take inside the app (added in version 3.0) — see below
  • App settings, such as whether reminders are on, and whether Premium is unlocked

This data is stored with Apple's on-device storage (SwiftData and UserDefaults). It is never transmitted to the developer or to any external server.

Camera and Proof Photos

Version 3.0 lets you take an optional photo of an item as you check it — for example the stove dials or a locked door. Camera access is requested only the first time you tap the camera button; if you decline, the app works exactly as before without photos.

  • Photos are taken inside the app and stored only in the app's own private storage on your iPhone. They are never saved to your photo library.
  • Location and other photo metadata (EXIF) are removed before a photo is stored.
  • Proof photos are excluded from iCloud and computer backups and are protected by iOS file encryption.
  • Photos are deleted automatically after the period you choose in Settings (1 day, 7 days by default, 30 days, or until you delete the check). Deleting a checklist or using Delete All Data deletes its photos.
  • Photos are never uploaded. A photo or a check leaves your iPhone only if you share it yourself using the iOS share sheet; shared check pictures contain the times and item names, not your photos.

Lock Screen, Live Activity, Siri and Shortcuts

After a check, the app can show its time on your Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island as a Live Activity. This is created on your iPhone; no push service or server is involved, and you can turn it off in the app's Settings.

Siri and the Shortcuts app can ask Before You Go when a checklist was last checked, or start a check (for example from an NFC tag or a Shortcuts automation you set up). These answers are read from your iPhone. Before You Go does not use your location for any of this — any automation trigger runs inside iOS.

Widgets

If you add a Before You Go widget, the app shares checklist names, icons, and last-check times with the widget through an App Group — a storage area on your iPhone that only Before You Go and its widget can read. Nothing leaves your device.

Notifications

Hotel checkout reminders and daily reminders are scheduled locally on your device using Apple's notification framework. Notification permission is requested only when you set up a reminder. No data is sent to any server to deliver them.

Payments and Purchases

The optional one-time Premium purchase is processed by Apple through the App Store (StoreKit). The app verifies the purchase on your device. The developer does not receive, process, or store your payment information or any personal data from the purchase. See Apple's privacy policy for purchase-related data handled by Apple.

Third-Party Services

This app does not include third-party analytics services, advertising networks, or tracking SDKs.

Data Sharing

The developer does not sell, trade, rent, or share user data with anyone. There is no developer-side database of user content.

Data Deletion

You can delete individual checklists inside the app, or use Settings › Delete All Data to remove every checklist, hotel stay, check history and proof photo. Deleting the app also removes all of its data from your device. Your Premium purchase stays with your Apple Account and can be restored at any time.

Children's Privacy

Before You Go does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

If the app's features change in a way that affects how information is handled, the updated policy will be posted on this page with a new "Last Updated" date.
