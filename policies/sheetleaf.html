Sheetleaf

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 15, 2026

Last Updated: 15 November 2026 (version 1.0)

Introduction

Sheetleaf turns an Excel workbook (.xlsx) you already use — a quote, a cost estimate, an order form, a price calculator — into a form on your iPhone. You fill in the values, the results are calculated on your device as you type, and you can export a new copy of the workbook with your values. Sheetleaf is free to download; the sample and one workbook of your own can be filled in and calculated without limit, and a single optional in-app purchase unlocks the rest, once and permanently. This app is designed to work without accounts, tracking, analytics, advertising, or developer-side storage of user-created content. Apart from that one purchase, which the App Store handles, Sheetleaf works entirely offline.

Information We Do Not Collect

This app does not collect personal information from users. Specifically, the developer does not collect:

  • Name, email address, phone number, or physical address
  • Your workbooks, the values you enter, or anything calculated from them
  • Contact list or address book data
  • Photos or media from your device
  • Precise or approximate location data
  • Financial account information or payment card information
  • Advertising identifiers or device identifiers used for tracking
  • Analytics or behavioral data
  • User-created content stored on developer servers

Your Workbooks

A workbook enters Sheetleaf only when you choose it in the standard iOS file picker; Sheetleaf cannot see any other file. It keeps its own copy of that workbook inside a project, reads it on your device, and never changes the file you chose. Workbooks are not uploaded, scanned by a server, or sent to anyone: there is no developer server, and no part of a workbook leaves your device unless you export or share it yourself.

Purchases

Sheetleaf offers one non-consumable in-app purchase, the Full Version, which unlocks any number of projects, exporting an edited copy of a workbook, and sharing a summary as a PDF. The purchase is made through Apple's App Store and is processed entirely by Apple. The developer never sees and never receives your name, your email address, your Apple Account or any payment card or account information. The app asks the App Store a single question — whether this Apple Account owns the Full Version — and stores nothing about the answer. Restoring a purchase asks the App Store the same question again. Apple's own handling of App Store transactions is described in Apple's privacy policy.

Sheetleaf contains no advertising, no subscriptions and no consumable purchases, and nothing in the app can be bought more than once.

Information Stored Locally on Your Device

Sheetleaf stores your projects on your device: for each one, the copy of the workbook you imported, the values you entered, the name you gave the project, and any corrections you made to how a field is shown. Projects are kept in the app's own folder, which the Files app shows under On My iPhone ▸ Sheetleaf, so you can see, copy or remove them yourself. None of this data is transmitted to the developer or any external server.

Exporting and Sharing

When you export an edited copy, Sheetleaf writes a new workbook to the folder you choose in the standard iOS file picker. When you share a copy or a summary PDF, Sheetleaf hands the file to the standard iOS share sheet. Where it goes is your choice, made in those system screens; Sheetleaf does not send anything itself, does not learn where a file went, and keeps no copy of a shared file after you close the screen.

App Permissions

Sheetleaf does not request access to sensitive device permissions such as location, camera, microphone, photos, contacts, or notifications. Files are reached only through the system file picker, one at a time, when you choose them.

Third-Party Services

This app does not integrate third-party analytics services, advertising networks, or tracking SDKs, and it contains no third-party code or libraries of any kind. The only network communication involving Sheetleaf is Apple's own, through its StoreKit framework: StoreKit reads the price of the Full Version and whether this Apple Account owns it, and it handles the purchase and restore described above. There is no developer server and no other network request of any kind: workbooks are read, calculated and exported on your own device. The app is available in English and Turkish; the language is chosen by your device settings and that choice never leaves your device.

Data Sharing

The developer does not sell, trade, rent, or share user data with third parties. There is no developer-side database of user-created content. Your information stays on your device.

Data Deletion

You can delete a project at any time in the app, or remove it in the Files app. Deleting the app removes all projects stored in it. Because nothing is stored anywhere else, there is no copy to recover once a project is deleted, except in backups of your device that iOS makes according to your own backup settings. Your purchase is held by Apple, not by the app, so it can always be restored.

Children's Privacy

This app does not knowingly collect personal information from children or from any users, as it does not collect personal information in general. The app is designed to function without any personal data being transmitted to the developer.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This privacy policy may be updated if the app's features change in a way that affects how information is handled. When updated, the revised policy will be posted on this page with a new effective date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

Contact
