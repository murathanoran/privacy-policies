How do I play?

Every board has a start dot and a goal ring. Find the shortest route between them, then draw it in one line without lifting your finger, touching a shape or leaving the board. Take as long as you like to plan: nothing moves and there is no timer. When you finish, the shortest route is drawn over yours.

How do stars work?

One star for reaching the ring. Two stars for taking the shortest route: the right side of every shape, and the right order through any diamonds. Three stars for drawing that route within about 2% of its shortest length. If two routes are equally short, either one counts. Stars never block progress: finishing a level always opens the next one.

What are diamonds, gates and keys?

Diamonds must all be passed before the ring counts, in whichever order you choose. Gates are one-way: the arrows show the only direction the line may cross them. A door blocks the line until the line has passed its key; a key is only worth picking up when the detour pays for itself.

I'm stuck. Is there a hint?

Yes, free and unlimited. After a few tries, Perfect Line offers to show the first move of the shortest route, and then the whole route as a dashed line. Hints are never sold. A level finished with the whole route shown earns up to two stars; draw it again on your own for the third.

What is the Daily Line?

One board a day, the same for everyone, built into the app so it works offline. A new one arrives at midnight in your time zone. Finish it on its own day to keep your streak. Perfect Line sends no reminders and no notifications.

What is free, and what is Perfect Line Complete?

Free, always: chapters 1 to 5 in full, the first two levels of every other chapter, today's Daily Line, hints, Relaxed Drawing and the share card.

Perfect Line Complete is a single, optional one-time purchase. It opens the rest of every chapter, including the gate and key chapters, and every past Daily Line. There is no subscription, no energy, no in-game currency and there are no ads.

I bought Perfect Line before it became free. Do I pay again?

No. Everyone who bought Perfect Line 1.x keeps everything automatically, including all the new chapters. If anything shows as locked, for example on a new device, open Settings ▸ Restore Purchase while signed in with the Apple Account you used.

I bought Perfect Line Complete and levels are still locked

Open Settings ▸ Restore Purchase. Make sure you are online and signed in with the Apple Account that made the purchase. If the purchase is waiting for approval (Ask to Buy), the game unlocks by itself as soon as it is approved.

Where did my old scores go?

Nowhere. Perfect Line 3.0 turned every level you had already finished into stars, using your best line where it was saved. No progress is lost.

Accessibility

Relaxed Drawing (Settings) lets the line brush the edge of a shape and makes diamonds and keys count from farther away. The puzzle and the stars stay the same. Perfect Line follows your text size, Reduce Motion and dark mode. VoiceOver describes each board and announces every result; drawing itself needs touch. On iPad, Apple Pencil works like a finger.

Sound and haptics

Both can be turned off in Settings. Perfect Line respects the silent switch and never stops your music.

Perfect Line has no account and collects no data. Read the full privacy policy.
