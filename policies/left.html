Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 23, 2026

Introduction

Left. is designed to help you manage simple daily or periodic budgets using an envelope-style approach. This app is designed to work without accounts, tracking, analytics, advertising, or developer-side storage of user-created content.

Information We Do Not Collect

This app does not collect personal information from users. Specifically, the developer does not collect:

  • Name, email address, phone number, or physical address
  • Contact list or address book data
  • Photos or media from your device, unless you explicitly choose a photo for an optional in-app feature
  • Precise or approximate location data, unless explicitly required by a feature you choose to use
  • Financial account information or payment card information
  • Advertising identifiers or device identifiers used for tracking
  • Analytics or behavioral data
  • User-created content stored on developer servers

Information Stored Locally on Your Device

Left. may store the following data locally on your device: budget envelope names, daily or periodic budget amounts, spending entries, categories, dates, display preferences, and general app settings. None of this data is transmitted to the developer or any external server.

App Permissions

Left. does not connect to banks, financial institutions, or payment processors. The app does not access your bank account information, credit card data, debit card data, or any financial account credentials. All budget information is entered manually by you within the app. No sensitive financial permission is requested.

Payments and Purchases

Purchases, if available within this app, are processed by Apple through the App Store using StoreKit. The developer does not receive, process, or store your payment card information. All payment handling is managed entirely by Apple.

Third-Party Services

This app does not integrate third-party analytics services, advertising networks, or tracking SDKs. If the app uses Apple platform features such as local notifications or in-app purchases through StoreKit, these are device-level or App Store platform features provided by Apple and are not used for tracking or profiling users.

Data Sharing

The developer does not sell, trade, rent, or share user data with third parties. There is no developer-side database of user-created content. Your information stays on your device.

Data Deletion

You can delete your data at any time by removing individual entries inside the app where this option is available, resetting app data through app settings where available, or deleting the app from your device. Deleting the app removes all locally stored data associated with it.

Children's Privacy

This app does not knowingly collect personal information from children or from any users, as it does not collect personal information in general. The app is designed to function without any personal data being transmitted to the developer.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This privacy policy may be updated if the app's features change in a way that affects how information is handled. When updated, the revised policy will be posted on this page with a new effective date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

Contact

For privacy-related inquiries regarding this application, please use the contact methods provided through the App Store listing.
