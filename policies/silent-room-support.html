What is free, and what is Silent Room Complete?

Free, always: 14 rooms, every noise colour (white, pink, brown, deep brown, green and grey), light and steady rain, wind, a fan, surf, the city at night and a quiet café; mixing up to three of those sounds; the full sleep timer with its long fade; Lock Screen playback controls, Siri and Control Center; your notes; and a 30-second preview of anything in Complete.

Silent Room Complete is a single, optional one-time purchase. It adds every room and sound (40 rooms and 38 sounds today, and everything added later), mixing up to six sounds, rooms of your own, the character control on each sound (a fan's speed, how hard the rain falls, a heartbeat's pace), warmth, the night plan with a gentle wake, the sleep timer on the Lock Screen, a dim bedside clock and Home Screen widgets. There is no subscription, and it is shared with your family through Family Sharing.

I bought Premium or Complete in an earlier version. Do I pay again?

No. Silent Room Complete is the same purchase as the earlier "Premium", so it unlocks automatically. If it doesn't, open Settings ▸ Restore purchase.

I bought Complete and things are still locked

Open Settings ▸ Restore purchase. It asks the App Store whether your Apple Account owns Silent Room Complete and tells you plainly either way. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple Account you bought it with (or one in the same Family Sharing group) and that you are online.

Can the night plan replace my alarm?

No. The night plan plays one room while you fall asleep, another through the night, and then lets a morning room rise slowly from almost nothing to full by your wake time. It can only do that while Silent Room is still playing. If the sound is stopped, paused for a long time or the app is closed, the morning room won't play. Keep your phone's alarm for anything that matters.

Do the sounds loop?

No. Every sound in Silent Room is generated live on your device as you listen, so there is no recording on repeat and no seam to hear, all night long.

The sound stopped when another app played audio

By default Silent Room has the audio to itself and shows its controls on the Lock Screen. To let a podcast or music play over your room, turn on Settings ▸ Play alongside other audio. Silent Room pauses when headphones disconnect; you can change that in Settings ▸ Pause when headphones disconnect.

How do I start a room without opening the app?

Say "Play Silent Room" to Siri, add the Silent Room control to Control Center (iOS 18 or later), or use the Home Screen widget (Silent Room Complete). The room you played last starts.

Which languages does Silent Room speak?

English, German, French, Spanish, Japanese, Portuguese (Brazil) and Turkish. Silent Room follows your device's language; you can choose a different one for Silent Room alone in the iPhone's Settings ▸ Apps ▸ Silent Room ▸ Language.
