How do I use it?

Search for a food or drink, or pick one from Popular or a category. Choose a serving — or type your own amount — and FoodMotion shows the same energy as minutes of walking, running, cycling and other activities. Tap All activities to see every one.

What is free, and what is FoodMotion Complete?

Free, always: every food and every activity, your own body weight, km or miles, kcal or kJ, distances, favourites, the share card, Siri and Shortcuts, and the "How this is estimated" page.

FoodMotion Complete is a single, optional one-time purchase. It adds whole meals, starting from an activity, your own foods, comparison history, and the share studio (story and square cards, more themes, up to four activities). There is no subscription and there are no ads.

I bought FoodMotion Complete and things are still locked

Open Settings ▸ Restore purchase. It asks the App Store whether your Apple Account owns FoodMotion Complete and tells you plainly either way. Make sure you are online and signed in with the same Apple Account.

How are the numbers worked out?

Each activity has a MET value from the 2024 Adult Compendium of Physical Activities. Energy per minute = MET × 3.5 × body weight (kg) ÷ 200. Food energy comes from USDA FoodData Central. Without your weight, a 70 kg reference adult is used. The full explanation is in the app under How this is estimated.

Why are my numbers different from my watch?

By default FoodMotion counts only the energy above sitting quietly — the extra energy a movement takes. Many fitness trackers show total energy, including what your body uses at rest. Turn on Settings ▸ Include resting energy to compare like with like. Fitness, technique, terrain and temperature also change real energy use, so every number is an estimate.

A food I eat is missing

With FoodMotion Complete you can add your own foods with their energy per serving, and they appear in search. You are also welcome to email me foods you would like to see in a future version.

Can I use Siri?

Yes. Say "Compare a food in FoodMotion", or use the Compare a food with walking action in the Shortcuts app. FoodMotion answers with a brisk walk for one serving, for a 70 kg reference adult.

How do I delete my data?

Settings ▸ Delete all my data removes everything you entered. Settings ▸ Clear history removes only the history, and Keep comparison history turns it off. Nothing is stored anywhere except your iPhone.

Privacy

FoodMotion works offline, has no account and collects nothing. Read the privacy policy.
