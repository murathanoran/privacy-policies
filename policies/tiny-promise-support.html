Common questions

How is my self-trust score worked out?

It is the share of promises you kept in the last 30 days, counting only promises you answered as kept or broken. Promises you let go, and promises still open, don't count either way.

What is free, and what is Pro?

One promise a day, timers, reminders, your self-trust score, streaks, reflections and your full history are free, with no trial and no time limit. Tiny Promise Pro is a one-time purchase that adds up to five promises a day, repeating promises, widgets, the Lock Screen countdown, Insights, the full calendar and CSV export.

I bought Tiny Promise before version 2.0.

Your purchase is Tiny Promise Pro. If it doesn't show, open Settings in the app and tap Restore Purchase, signed in with the same Apple Account.

How do I add a widget?

Widgets are part of Tiny Promise Pro. Touch and hold your Home Screen, tap Edit, then Add Widget, and choose Tiny Promise. For the Lock Screen, touch and hold it and tap Customize.

Can I answer without opening the app?

Yes. When a timed promise's time is up, touch and hold the notification and choose I kept it or I broke it.

How do repeating promises work?

Choose Repeat when you make a promise (Pro). A fresh promise appears on each day you picked, running all day or until the time you set. Pause or delete repeating promises in Settings; promises already made stay in your history.

My reminders don't arrive.

Check Settings > Notifications > Tiny Promise on your iPhone and make sure notifications are allowed, then turn the reminder on again in the app.

How do I delete my data?

In the app, open Settings and tap Delete all promises, or delete the app.
