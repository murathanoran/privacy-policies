Where Was I?

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: June 23, 2026

Last Updated: September 25, 2026

Introduction

Where Was I? keeps your place in the shows, books, podcasts, courses and other things you are in the middle of. Everything you enter stays on your iPhone. The developer does not collect it, cannot see it, and there is no server for it to go to.

Information We Do Not Collect

Where Was I? has no account and no sign-up. It collects no personal data, no usage data, no analytics, no crash reports, no identifiers and no location. It contains no advertising and no third-party code.

What Where Was I? Stores on Your Device

The titles you add, your place in each (for example season and episode, page, chapter, lesson or a timestamp), the notes and recaps you write, ratings, categories you create, reminder times, and a timeline of the places you saved. This is stored only on your iPhone, in the app's own protected storage, and is included in your iPhone's own iCloud or computer backup if you use one — exactly like any other app's data. The developer has no access to those backups.

App Permissions

Notifications — asked for only when you set a reminder on an entry. Reminders are scheduled on the iPhone itself; no notification is sent from a server. Where Was I? asks for no other permission: no camera, photos, location, contacts, calendar or microphone.

Widgets

Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets read your list from a storage area on your iPhone that the app shares only with its own widget, so they can show your place and move it forward. Nothing leaves the device.

Backups

You can export a backup file from Settings. The file is created on your iPhone and saved wherever you choose (for example in Files). The app never uploads it. Restoring reads a file you pick and adds what is missing; it never deletes anything.

Payments and Purchases

Where Was I Unlimited is a one-time in-app purchase handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. The developer never receives your name, Apple Account or payment details. To sell and restore the purchase, the app talks to the App Store — this is the only network connection it makes.

Third-Party Services

None. The app uses only Apple's own frameworks.

Data Sharing

Nothing is shared with anyone, because nothing is collected.

Data Deletion

Delete an entry in the app, or delete the app to remove all of its data from your iPhone. Backup files you exported are yours to delete.

Children's Privacy

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
