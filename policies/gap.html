GAP

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 21, 2026

Last Updated: September 13, 2026

Introduction

GAP is a minimalist one-tap arcade game: rings rotate around a central dot, and the aim is to tap at the exact moment their openings line up so the dot can pass through every ring. This app is designed to work without accounts, tracking, analytics, advertising, or developer-side storage of user-created content. GAP works entirely offline; the only network activity is the optional in-app purchase, which is handled by Apple.

Information We Do Not Collect

This app does not collect personal information from users. Specifically, the developer does not collect:

  • Name, email address, phone number, or physical address
  • Contact list or address book data
  • Photos or media from your device
  • Precise or approximate location data
  • Financial account information or payment card information
  • Advertising identifiers or device identifiers used for tracking
  • Analytics or behavioral data
  • User-created content stored on developer servers

Information Stored Locally on Your Device

GAP may store the following data locally on your device: your level progress and which levels you have completed; your attempt counts, first-try completions and best streak; your best Endless run and how many runs you have played; your daily challenge streak and which days you have completed; whether the one-time request to rate the app has already been shown; whether this device owns the in-app purchase; and your app settings and preferences (haptics, sound effects, appearance, reduced motion, timing feedback). None of this data is transmitted to the developer or any external server.

App Permissions

GAP does not request access to sensitive device permissions such as location, camera, microphone, photos, or contacts. The app is fully functional offline and does not require any special device permissions to operate.

Third-Party Services

This app does not integrate third-party analytics services, advertising networks, or tracking SDKs. Apart from the in-app purchase, which Apple handles, GAP does not connect to the internet, and all gameplay logic runs entirely on your device.

Payments and Purchases

GAP is free to download and contains no advertising. It offers a single one-time purchase, GAP Complete, which unlocks the rest of the campaign and Endless mode. It is processed by Apple through the App Store using StoreKit. The developer does not receive, process, or store your payment card information. All payment handling is managed entirely by Apple. There is no subscription, and the purchase is never sold twice.

Data Sharing

The developer does not sell, trade, rent, or share user data with third parties. There is no developer-side database of user-created content. Your information stays on your device.

Data Deletion

You can delete your data at any time by resetting your progress through the app's Settings screen, or by deleting the app from your device. Deleting the app removes all locally stored data associated with it.

Children's Privacy

This app does not knowingly collect personal information from children or from any users, as it does not collect personal information in general. The app is designed to function without any personal data being transmitted to the developer.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This privacy policy may be updated if the app's features change in a way that affects how information is handled. When updated, the revised policy will be posted on this page with a new effective date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
