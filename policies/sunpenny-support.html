Common questions

Is there a subscription?

No. Sunpenny Premium is a single one-time purchase; nothing renews and nothing is charged automatically. A new install gets a free 7-day trial of every feature. The daily number and logging expenses in the app stay free for good.

How do I restore my purchase on a new device?

Sign in with the same Apple Account, then open Settings › Restore Purchase (just below Sunpenny Premium). It is also on the Premium page, and on the first setup screen under Already using Sunpenny? › Restore purchase. With Family Sharing, members of your family group can use the purchase too.

How do I redeem a code?

Open Settings › Redeem Code. Apple's own code sheet opens; enter the code you received.

Where is my data?

On your device. If you turn on Settings › iCloud Sync (a Premium feature, off until you turn it on), it is also kept in your own private iCloud. There is no Sunpenny server and no account. Settings › Where Your Data Lives shows where it is right now. See the privacy policy.

How do backups work?

Settings › Back Up makes an encrypted backup file whenever you like; you choose where it goes. Settings › Where Your Data Lives › Automatic Backups lets Sunpenny make one by itself once a week and when a new budget starts, keeping the newest five. You choose the passphrase; only it opens them. Find them in the Files app under On My iPhone › Sunpenny › Automatic Backups (On My iPad on an iPad). To bring one back, use Settings › Restore from Backup.

Can I bring my spending from another app?

Yes. Export a CSV file from the other app or your bank, then use Settings › Import Expenses from CSV. You can pick which column is the date, amount, note and category, choose the date format and decimal separator, and see a preview before anything is added. Expenses in your current budget are added to it; earlier ones become finished budgets in History. The same expense is never added twice, and Undo is offered right after the import.

Can Apple Pay purchases be logged automatically?

On iPhone, yes — with a Shortcuts automation you set up yourself. Settings › Siri & Shortcuts › Log Apple Pay Automatically shows the steps. Sunpenny never sees your cards or your bank.

How do I start over?

Settings › Erase All Data. It deletes every budget, expense, bill and setting on this device, and the automatic backups and their passphrase on this device too — so copy a backup somewhere safe first if you may want it. With iCloud Sync on, it also deletes your data from iCloud and from your other devices. To erase only one device, choose Erase This iPhone Only… (or iPad) in the same dialog: it turns iCloud Sync off first, and iCloud and your other devices keep everything. Your purchase stays with your Apple Account.

Sunpenny used to have a different name.

Yes. It is the same app with the same data and the same purchase, under a new name.
