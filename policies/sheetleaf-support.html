Support

Contact

Email murathanoran1@gmail.com. One person reads it, usually within two business days. Telling me which iPhone you have, your iOS version and what you were doing when it happened makes it much easier to help. Please do not send a workbook unless I ask for it, and never one with data you would not share.

Which files can I open?

Excel workbooks in the .xlsx format. Old .xls workbooks and workbooks encrypted with a password are not opened; save them as .xlsx in Excel first.

Where are my projects?

On your iPhone. The Files app shows them under On My iPhone ▸ Sheetleaf. The workbook you imported is never changed; Sheetleaf works on its own copy.

A result is marked as not calculated

The workbook uses something Sheetleaf does not calculate yet. The reason is shown with the result, and the workbook's saved value is kept and shown struck through once it would have changed. Sheetleaf never shows a guess as a calculated result.

Why can't I export this workbook?

Workbooks with macros, PivotTables, data connections or links to other workbooks can be viewed and calculated, but an edited copy cannot be written safely, so export is turned off for them. The project's report says which feature it is.

What is free, and what does the Full Version add?

Free: the sample, copies of the sample, and one workbook of your own — with unlimited filling in and calculating. Nothing is taken away if you never buy anything.

One optional payment, made once, adds any number of projects, Export Edited Copy… and Share Summary PDF…. There is no subscription and nothing else to buy.

I paid, and the app still shows the free version

Open Projects ▸ Full Version ▸ Restore Purchase. That asks the App Store whether your Apple Account owns the Full Version.

If it says nothing was found and you are sure you paid: check that you are signed in with the same Apple Account you bought it with, and that you have a network connection, since the App Store cannot be asked without one. If it still refuses, email me with the date of the purchase and I will look into it.

Refunds

Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Does Sheetleaf work offline?

Yes. Workbooks are read, calculated and exported on your own device. A network is needed only for the one purchase and for restoring it.

Accessibility

Sheetleaf follows your text size up to the largest accessibility sizes and supports Dark Mode. If something is unusable for you — with VoiceOver or in any other way — please write: that is a bug, and it is treated as one.
