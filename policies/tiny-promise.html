Tiny Promise

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: June 23, 2026

Last Updated: September 30, 2026

Introduction

Tiny Promise helps you make small promises to yourself and answer honestly whether you kept them. Everything you enter stays on your iPhone. The developer does not collect it, cannot see it, and there is no server for it to go to.

Information We Do Not Collect

Tiny Promise has no account and no sign-up. It collects no personal data, no usage data, no analytics, no crash reports, no identifiers and no location. It contains no advertising and no third-party code.

What Tiny Promise Stores on Your Device

The promises you write, their dates and time windows, whether you kept, broke or let go of each one, the reasons and notes you add when a promise is broken, your repeating promises, your reminder times and your app settings. This is stored only on your iPhone, in the app's own protected storage, and is included in your iPhone's own iCloud or computer backup if you use one — exactly like any other app's data. The developer has no access to those backups.

App Permissions

Notifications — asked for only when you turn on a reminder or make a timed promise. Reminders and "Time's up" notifications are scheduled on the iPhone itself; no notification is sent from a server. You can hide the text of your promises from notifications, the Lock Screen countdown and Lock Screen widgets in Settings. Tiny Promise asks for no other permission: no camera, photos, location, contacts, calendar or microphone.

Widgets and Live Activities

To show today's promises and your self-trust score, the app writes a small summary to a storage area on your iPhone that it shares only with its own widget. The Lock Screen countdown for a timed promise is shown by iOS on the device. Nothing leaves the iPhone.

Export

With Tiny Promise Pro you can export your history as a CSV file. The file is created on your iPhone and goes only where you choose to share or save it. The app never uploads it.

Payments and Purchases

Tiny Promise Pro is a one-time in-app purchase handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. The developer never receives your name, Apple Account or payment details. To sell and restore the purchase, the app talks to the App Store — this is the only network connection it makes. A small "Pro purchased" flag is kept in the iOS Keychain on your iPhone so the purchase is recognised again after reinstalling.

Third-Party Services

None. The app uses only Apple's own frameworks.

Data Sharing

Nothing is shared with anyone, because nothing is collected.

Data Deletion

Delete a single promise in the app, use Delete all promises in Settings, or delete the app to remove your promises from your iPhone. Only the "Pro purchased" flag described above stays in the Keychain. CSV files you exported are yours to delete.

Children's Privacy

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
