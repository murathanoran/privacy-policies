How do I play?

Watch the obstacles move. They repeat, so every level has moments when the path from the start to the target is clear. Predict one of those moments and tap once: the Core travels straight up the line and either passes or is blocked. PASS is not a reflex test. A good read beats a fast thumb, and you can watch for as long as you like before you tap.

What does EARLY or LATE mean after a miss?

That is the Read Line. After a blocked attempt it shows where your tap landed compared with the nearest safe moment, and by how much. It tells you why the attempt failed, not when to tap next.

If you miss the same level five times, PASS can offer a live Read Line that marks the safe moments in the repeating cycle. It only appears if you ask for it, and you can turn it off for good in Settings ▸ Read Line hints. It is never used in the Daily Read.

What is Perfect?

A pass right in the middle of a safe moment. Perfect is optional mastery: it never blocks progress and, once earned, is never lost.

What is the Daily Read?

A new puzzle every day, built on your iPhone from the date, so there is nothing to download. Your result is the number of attempts it took to pass; one is the best. The puzzles get harder through the week, with Monday the gentlest and Sunday the hardest. A new one arrives at midnight in your time zone. You can replay a solved Daily Read as often as you like, but replays do not change your result. The Daily Read is free for everyone.

How does the streak work?

Every day you pass the Daily Read counts. Missing a day starts the streak over. PASS sends no reminders and no notifications.

What is free, and what is the full game?

Free, always: levels 1 to 45 (the first three bands: Fundamentals, Prediction and Synchronization), the Daily Read with sharing, and your Record. If you played PASS 2.0, you keep levels 1 to 50 for free, as before.

The full game is a single, optional one-time purchase. It adds the remaining bands, up to level 150, and Endless mode. There is no subscription, no in-game currency and there are no ads.

I bought PASS when it was a paid app. Do I pay again?

No. Everyone who bought PASS 1.x keeps the full game automatically. If anything shows as locked, for example on a new iPhone, open Settings ▸ Restore Purchase while signed in with the Apple Account you bought PASS with.

I bought the full game and levels are still locked

Open Settings ▸ Restore Purchase. Make sure you are online and signed in with the Apple Account that made the purchase. If the purchase is waiting for approval (Ask to Buy), the game unlocks by itself as soon as it is approved.

What does Game Center do?

Nothing, unless you turn it on in Settings ▸ Game Center. When it is on, your longest Daily Read streak and your best Endless score are posted to Apple's Game Center leaderboards, and Leaderboards opens Apple's own leaderboard screen. Turn the switch off and PASS stops posting immediately. PASS itself still collects nothing.

What is the Record screen?

A summary of what you have done: levels passed and Perfect, levels read on the first try, Daily Read results and streaks, your best Endless run, and how far you are through each band. First tries are counted from version 3.0 onward, so levels you passed before 3.0 are not included.

A tap did not seem to register

PASS times the tap from the moment your finger touches the screen, not from the next frame. A tap during a pass or a block animation is ignored on purpose, so one tap can never start two attempts. If a tap is ignored at any other time, please email the level number and your iPhone model.

Sound, haptics and accessibility

Sound and haptics can be turned off in Settings. PASS respects the silent switch and never interrupts your music. It follows your text size, and with Reduce Motion on, the decorative animations stop moving while the obstacles, which are the game, still move. With VoiceOver, the playfield is a single control: double-tap to launch. After a miss, VoiceOver says whether the tap was early or late.
