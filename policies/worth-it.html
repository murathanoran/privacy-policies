Worth It

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 27, 2026

Last Updated: October 8, 2026 (version 2.0)

The short version

Worth It is a private record of your decisions: what you expected when you made them, and what actually happened. It has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and it never connects to a server run by the developer. Everything you write stays on your iPhone. The developer cannot read your decisions, your verdicts or your lessons, because they are never sent anywhere.

Need help instead? See the Worth It support page.

Information we do not collect

The developer does not collect, receive or store any of the following:

  • Name, email address, phone number or physical address
  • Contacts, photos, location, microphone or camera input (Worth It uses none of these)
  • Health data of any kind
  • Payment card or financial account information
  • Advertising identifiers or device identifiers used for tracking
  • Analytics, crash reports collected by the developer, or usage behaviour
  • Anything you write in the app

Information stored on your iPhone

To work, Worth It keeps the following on your device only:

  • The decisions you record: title, category, why you made it, what you expected, dates, an optional note, an optional cost, and an optional "how sure are you" rating
  • Your verdicts: what happened, whether it was worth it, whether you would do it again, your lesson and advice to your past self — including any later "still worth it?" looks
  • Review and revisit dates, and your settings (reminder time, appearance, export format)

The Hindsight report is calculated on your iPhone from this data each time you open it; it is not stored or sent anywhere.

If you add a Worth It widget, a small summary (your next look back, how many decisions are ready, and your written lessons) is shared with Worth It's own widgets through an App Group on your device so the widgets can show it. Lock Screen widgets are marked private, so iOS can hide their text while your iPhone is locked. None of this information is transmitted to the developer or to any third party. Your iPhone's own backups may include it, as they do for any app.

Notifications (optional)

When you record a decision, Worth It offers one reminder on the day you chose to look back, and one more if you schedule a "still worth it?" revisit. iOS asks for permission the first time. Reminders are scheduled locally on your iPhone at the time of day you choose in Settings, and the reminder text includes the decision's title. No notification is sent from a server. You can turn reminders off in Worth It's Settings at any time.

Exporting

You can export everything you wrote as a JSON or CSV file from Settings. The file is created on your iPhone and shared only where you choose to send it. Worth It deletes its temporary copy afterwards.

Purchases

Worth It Unlimited is a one-time purchase processed by Apple through the App Store. The developer never receives your payment details. The app asks the App Store whether your Apple Account owns the purchase so it can unlock it on your devices.

Third parties

Worth It contains no third-party SDKs, analytics services, advertising networks or tracking code. The developer does not sell, rent, trade or share any user data, because no user data is collected.

Deleting your data

You can delete any decision (and everything written about it) from the Decisions list or from the decision itself, and remove later looks individually. Deleting the app removes everything it stored on your iPhone.

Children

Worth It does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children, because it does not collect personal information at all.

Not financial advice

Worth It helps you reflect on your own decisions. It does not give financial, legal or medical advice, and its Hindsight figures describe only what you recorded.

Changes to this policy

If a future version changes how information is handled, this page will be updated and the date above will change.
