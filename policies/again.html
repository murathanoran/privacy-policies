Again

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: June 23, 2026

Last Updated: October 3, 2026

Introduction

Again remembers what you thought of the things you choose — a dish, a drink, a product, a film, a book — so the next time you are at the menu or the shelf you know whether to have it again. The app works without accounts, tracking, analytics, advertising or any developer-side storage. Everything you create stays on your iPhone.

Information We Do Not Collect

The developer does not collect any information about you. Specifically, the developer does not receive:

  • Your entries, verdicts, notes, photos, places or any other content you create
  • Name, email address, phone number or physical address
  • Contacts or address book data
  • Precise or approximate location — the app never asks for location access
  • Payment card or financial account information
  • Advertising identifiers or device identifiers used for tracking
  • Analytics, usage or crash data from inside the app

Information Stored on Your Device

Again stores the following only on your iPhone, in the app's private storage:

  • Your entries: what you had, your verdict (Again, Maybe or Not Again), category, reasons, a "next time" tip, notes, the barcode you linked to it, dates, and the history of your verdicts
  • Places you add to entries: the name, and the address and map coordinates of the Apple Maps result you picked
  • Photos you add to entries, saved as JPEG images of up to 2048 pixels
  • App settings, including how many free scans you have used

None of this is transmitted to the developer or to any server operated by the developer.

Barcode and Label Scanning

When you tap Scan, the app uses Apple's on-device VisionKit and Vision frameworks to read a barcode or the text on a label and look it up in your own entries. The camera is used only while the scanner is open. Camera images are not saved, not added to your photo library and not sent anywhere. Barcodes are matched only against your own entries; the app does not use any online product database.

Place Search

When you search for a place to add to an entry, the text you type is sent to Apple Maps (MapKit) to find matching places. This happens only while you search. The app does not access your location. Apple's handling of map searches is described in Apple's privacy policy.

iPhone Search, Siri and Shortcuts

To let you find a verdict from iPhone Search, the app adds your entries to the Spotlight index on your iPhone. This index stays on the device; you can turn it off in Settings → Search, which removes it. If you ask Siri or use a Shortcut to check a verdict, the app looks up the answer on your iPhone; your request to Siri is handled by Apple under Apple's terms.

Backups, Exports and Sharing

You can create a backup file of your data and choose where to save or send it (for example Files, iCloud Drive or AirDrop), and restore it later. Share cards for a verdict are created on your iPhone and are shared only when you choose to share them. Where these files go after you share them is up to you.

App Permissions

  • Camera — requested only the first time you tap Scan, used only while scanning.
  • Photos — the app uses the system photo picker, which gives the app only the photos you pick. The app never asks for access to your whole library.

The app does not request location, contacts, microphone, notifications or tracking permission.

Payments and Purchases

Again Premium is a one-time purchase processed by Apple through the App Store using StoreKit. The developer does not receive, process or store your payment information.

Third-Party Services

The app contains no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking code. It uses only Apple platform services: StoreKit for purchases, MapKit for place search, and on-device frameworks for scanning, Spotlight and Siri.

Data Sharing

The developer does not sell, trade, rent or share any data, because the developer does not have any.

Data Deletion

You can delete any entry inside the app, delete everything at once in Settings → Delete All Data, or delete the app. Deleting the app removes all of its data from your iPhone, including photos and its iPhone Search index. Backup files you saved elsewhere are not affected.

Children's Privacy

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This policy will be updated if the app changes in a way that affects how information is handled. The revised policy will be posted on this page with a new date.
