How Worth It works

1. Decide on the record. Write what you're deciding, why, and what you expect. Optionally weigh up to four options with pros and cons (weight 1–3), write what would tell you it was a mistake, and how sure you are.

2. Check in (optional) while it plays out: on track, not sure, or off track.

3. Look back on the day you chose. Worth It shows your own words first, then asks what happened, whether it was worth it, and — separately — whether the call itself was a good one.

4. Learn how you decide. Hindsight shows your accuracy, which outcomes were good calls and which were luck, and how mood, energy and your pros-and-cons lists relate to your results.

What is free, and what is Worth It Unlimited?

Free, always: up to five decisions; options with weighted pros and cons; mistake signals; mood and energy; reminders; looking back (early, on time, or on a decision that already happened); judging the call apart from the outcome; editing what you wrote; your past lessons when you record a similar decision; the Hindsight headline and your accuracy score; your lessons list; the examples; Siri and Shortcuts; the app lock; Spotlight; and exporting and importing everything.

Worth It Unlimited is a single, optional one-time purchase. It adds unlimited decisions, the full Hindsight report (calibration, good calls vs. good luck, mood and energy patterns, what pays off for you, surprises and letdowns), follow-through (check-ins and "still worth it?" revisits), the Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets, and the PDF Hindsight report. There is no subscription, no tokens and no ads.

I bought the unlock in an earlier version. Do I pay again?

No. Worth It Unlimited uses the same purchase as before, so it unlocks automatically. If it doesn't, open Settings ▸ Restore Purchase.

I bought Unlimited and things are still locked

Open Settings ▸ Restore Purchase. It asks the App Store whether your Apple Account owns Worth It Unlimited and tells you plainly either way. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple Account you bought it with and that you are online. If a parent needs to approve the purchase (Ask to Buy), Worth It unlocks by itself once it's approved.

Do the examples count toward my five free decisions?

No. The three examples (from See how it works with 3 examples on the empty Decisions screen) never count toward the limit, never appear in Hindsight, widgets or exports, and never send reminders. They can't be edited. Remove them any time with Remove examples.

I already had more than five decisions

Nothing you wrote is ever locked. You can keep reading, editing and looking back on every decision. Only adding a new one needs Worth It Unlimited.

Why can't I change my confidence after looking back?

Once you've looked back, what you predicted — how sure you were, your options, your mistake signal, your mood and energy — stays exactly as you wrote it. Otherwise answers given with hindsight would quietly improve your accuracy score. You can still edit the title, dates, cost, note and your verdict.

What is the accuracy score?

It measures how well your confidence matched what happened, from 0 to 100. Being sure and right scores high; being sure and wrong scores low; always saying "could go either way" scores about 50, a coin flip. It is calculated on your iPhone (from the Brier score used in forecasting) after at least three look backs on decisions you rated. Decisions logged afterwards aren't rated, because hindsight would bias the answer.

Moving to a new iPhone

Worth It has no account or cloud by design. Your decisions move with an iPhone backup or Quick Start like any app. You can also open Settings ▸ Export Everything with the format set to JSON, keep the file (for example in Files), and on the new iPhone choose Settings ▸ Import from a Backup. Importing never overwrites or duplicates a decision that is already there.

App lock

Turn on Settings ▸ Lock with Face ID (or Touch ID / passcode). While it's on, Worth It is hidden in the app switcher, notifications and widgets don't show your decisions, and nothing appears in Spotlight. If your iPhone ever loses its passcode, Worth It turns the lock off rather than lock you out of your own decisions, and tells you.

Reminders arrive at the wrong time, or not at all

Reminders arrive on the day you chose, at the time set in Settings ▸ Remind me at (9:00 by default). If none arrive, make sure Reminders is on in Worth It's Settings and that notifications for Worth It are allowed in the iPhone's Settings ▸ Notifications. Even without a reminder, the decision moves to "Ready to look back" on its day.

How do I add a widget?

Touch and hold an empty spot on your Home Screen, tap Edit ▸ Add Widget, search for Worth It, and choose "A Lesson From Past You", "Next Look Back" or "Your Accuracy". For the Lock Screen, touch and hold it, tap Customize, choose the Lock Screen and add Worth It in the widget area.

Siri and Shortcuts

Say "Record a decision in Worth It" or "What's next in Worth It", or find Worth It's actions in the Shortcuts app. Recording opens the app on a new decision — Worth It never writes a decision for you.

Languages

Worth It is available in English, Turkish, German, French, Spanish, Japanese and Brazilian Portuguese, following your iPhone's language.

Privacy

Worth It has no account, uses no AI and collects no data. Your decisions stay on your iPhone. Read the full privacy policy.
