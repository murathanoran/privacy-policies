One Photo Later

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 29, 2026

Last Updated: September 29, 2026 (version 2.0)

Introduction

One Photo Later lets you photograph things you want to come back to — something to buy, read, watch, try, visit or do — and reminds you about them later. The app works without accounts, tracking, analytics, advertising or any developer-side storage. Everything you create stays on your iPhone.

Information We Do Not Collect

The developer does not collect any information from you. Specifically, the developer does not receive:

  • Your photos, notes, titles, reminders or any other content you create
  • Name, email address, phone number or physical address
  • Contacts, location, health or financial data
  • Advertising identifiers or any device identifier used for tracking
  • Analytics, crash analytics or usage data

The app makes no network requests of its own. The only connection it uses is Apple's App Store (StoreKit) when you view, buy or restore the optional purchase.

Information Stored on Your Device

The app stores the following only on your iPhone, inside the app's private storage:

  • The photos you save, re-encoded at up to 2560 pixels
  • Titles, notes, what each photo is for, the reminder time you choose, lists, and whether an item is finished
  • Text recognised in your photos (see below), so you can search it
  • App settings, such as your reminder times

Photos are stored with iOS Data Protection (encrypted while your iPhone is locked) and are excluded from iCloud and computer backups. They are therefore not synced between devices, and the developer cannot recover them if you delete the app. Titles, notes and settings may be included in your own encrypted iCloud or computer backup of your iPhone, like any app's data; the developer has no access to those backups.

To show your photos in the Home Screen widget and to receive images from the Share button, the app keeps small thumbnails, a short summary of upcoming items and images waiting to be imported in an App Group container on your iPhone that only One Photo Later and its own extensions can read. This data never leaves the device.

Text Recognition

The app uses Apple's on-device Vision framework to read text in your photos — for example a book title, a price, a link or a date — so it can suggest a title, make the photo searchable and offer one-tap actions. This happens entirely on your iPhone. No image or recognised text is sent to the developer, to Apple for this purpose, or to any other service.

App Permissions

  • Camera — requested only when you tap Snap, to take the photo you want to save. Photos you take in the app are not added to your photo library.
  • Photos — the app uses the system photo picker, which shows your library in a separate, private process and gives the app only the photos you pick. The app never asks for access to your whole library.
  • Notifications — requested only the first time you set a reminder time. Reminders are scheduled locally on your iPhone and include a small copy of the photo so you can recognise it. No push notification service is used.

You can change any of these at any time in the iOS Settings app. The app keeps working without them.

Share Extension and Widget

When you send an image to One Photo Later with the Share button, the extension saves a copy into the app's private App Group storage on your iPhone; the app imports it the next time it opens. The widget reads only the on-device summary described above. Neither sends data anywhere.

Payments and Purchases

The optional one-time Premium purchase is processed by Apple through the App Store. The developer does not receive or store your payment information. The app checks your purchase using Apple's StoreKit on your device.

Third-Party Services

The app contains no third-party code: no analytics, advertising, crash-reporting or tracking SDKs. It uses only Apple system frameworks.

Data Sharing

The developer does not sell, trade, rent or share any data, because the developer does not have any. You can choose to share an individual photo yourself using the Share button inside the app.

Data Deletion

Delete a single photo from its detail screen, clear finished items from the Archive, or remove everything with Settings › Delete All Data inside the app. Deleting the app from your iPhone removes all of its data.

Children's Privacy

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

If a future version changes how information is handled, this page will be updated with a new effective date before that version is released.
