Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 6, 2026

Last Updated: October 6, 2026

Introduction

100 helps you do one thing a hundred times and keep a record of each one. You choose an activity, and every time you do it you fill one of a hundred units on a grid, optionally with a short note or a photo. There are no streaks, no deadlines, and nothing that resets or expires. The app works without accounts, tracking, analytics, advertising, or any developer-side storage of what you create. Everything you record stays on your iPhone.

Information We Do Not Collect

The developer does not collect any personal information. Specifically, the developer does not collect:

  • Name, email address, phone number, or physical address
  • Contact list or address book data
  • Your photos, notes, or anything else you record in the app
  • Precise or approximate location data
  • Financial account information or payment card information
  • Advertising identifiers or device identifiers used for tracking
  • Analytics, usage, crash, or behavioral data

Information Stored Only on Your Device

Everything you create is written to the app's private storage on your iPhone:

  • your hundreds: the name and symbol you choose, the dates they were started and finished, your optional reminder settings, and whether one is a second or later round;
  • each repetition you record: its date and time, an optional note you write, and an optional photo you add;
  • whether you have seen the welcome screen, and whether 100 Premium is unlocked on this device.

Photos. If you add a photo to a repetition (100 Premium), the app saves its own copy, reduced in size and re-encoded with all location, camera and time metadata removed. Choosing a photo from your library uses Apple's photo picker, which gives 100 only the photo you pick and no access to the rest of your library. Taking a photo uses the camera only when you choose "Take Photo". Photos are never uploaded.

Widgets and Siri. To show your progress in widgets, and to record a repetition from a widget or with Siri and Shortcuts, the app keeps a small summary (the name, symbol and current count of the hundreds you have in progress) and any repetitions waiting to be added, in a private container shared only between 100 and its own widget on the same device.

Backups. Your data is included in your iPhone's own iCloud or computer backup, under your control, like any other app's data. 100 does not use iCloud sync, and none of this data is transmitted to the developer or any external server.

App Permissions

100 asks for at most two permissions, each only at the moment it is needed and never when the app is first opened:

  • Notifications — only if you switch on a reminder for one of your hundreds. Reminders are local notifications created and delivered by your own device.
  • Camera — only when you choose "Take Photo".

Declining either changes nothing else: every other part of the app keeps working, and you can still add photos from your library. 100 never asks for location, contacts, microphone, health data, or tracking permission.

Purchases

100 Premium is a one-time in-app purchase, not a subscription. It is processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. The developer never receives your payment details or your Apple Account information; the app only learns from Apple whether Premium has been purchased on your Apple Account, so that it can unlock it and restore it on your other devices. If you bought 100 before it became free, the app checks the App Store receipt on your device to recognise that, so you never pay twice. This is the only time the app communicates with anything outside your iPhone, and it is with Apple.

Third-Party Services

This app does not integrate third-party analytics services, advertising networks, or tracking SDKs, and it contains no third-party code or libraries. Apart from Apple's own App Store services for the in-app purchase, everything the app does runs on your device.

Data Sharing

The developer does not sell, trade, rent, or share user data with third parties, and there is no developer-side database of user-created content. Sharing happens only when you choose it — for example when you share a progress card, a mosaic image, or a CSV export through the iOS share sheet. What happens to it then is up to you and the app you share it with.

Data Deletion

You can delete a single repetition, a note, a photo, or a whole hundred from within the app; deleting a hundred also deletes every repetition, note and photo recorded for it. Deleting the app removes all of its data from your device, including the summary used by the widget. Because nothing is stored anywhere else, there is no copy to recover once it is deleted.

Children's Privacy

This app does not knowingly collect personal information from children or from any users, as it does not collect personal information in general.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This privacy policy may be updated if the app's features change in a way that affects how information is handled. When updated, the revised policy will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
