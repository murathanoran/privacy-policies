Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 25, 2026

Last Updated: August 25, 2026

Introduction

100 is a minimalist app for doing one thing a hundred times: you choose an activity, and every time you do it you fill one of a hundred units on a grid. There are no streaks, no deadlines, and nothing that resets or expires. This app is designed to work without accounts, tracking, analytics, advertising, or developer-side storage of user-created content. 100 works entirely offline.

Information We Do Not Collect

This app does not collect personal information from users. Specifically, the developer does not collect:

  • Name, email address, phone number, or physical address
  • Contact list or address book data
  • Photos or media from your device
  • Precise or approximate location data
  • Financial account information or payment card information
  • Advertising identifiers or device identifiers used for tracking
  • Analytics or behavioral data
  • User-created content stored on developer servers

Information Stored Locally on Your Device

100 may store the following data locally on your device: the name you give each challenge and the optional symbol you choose for it; the date each challenge was created; the date and time of every repetition you record; the date a challenge reached one hundred; your optional reminder settings for each challenge; and whether you have seen the welcome screen. This is written to a database inside the app's own storage area. If you add the home screen or lock screen widget, a small summary — the name, symbol and current count of your most recently updated challenges — is also written to a private container shared between the app and its widget on the same device, so that the widget can display your progress. None of this data is transmitted to the developer or any external server.

App Permissions

100 does not request access to sensitive device permissions such as location, camera, microphone, photos, contacts, or health data. The app asks for permission to send notifications only if you choose to switch on a reminder for a challenge, and only at that moment — never when the app is first opened. Reminders are entirely optional: if you decline, or later turn notifications off in iOS Settings, every other part of the app continues to work exactly as before. Any reminder you do schedule is a local notification created and delivered by your own device.

Third-Party Services

This app does not integrate third-party analytics services, advertising networks, or tracking SDKs, and it contains no third-party code, libraries, or bundled assets of any kind. 100 does not connect to the internet at all: everything the app does runs entirely on your device.

Data Sharing

The developer does not sell, trade, rent, or share user data with third parties. There is no developer-side database of user-created content. Your information stays on your device.

Data Deletion

You can delete your data at any time by deleting an individual challenge from within the app, which also deletes every repetition recorded for it, or by deleting the app from your device. Deleting the app removes all locally stored data associated with it, including the summary used by the widget. Because nothing is stored anywhere else, there is no copy of your challenges to recover once they are deleted.

Children's Privacy

This app does not knowingly collect personal information from children or from any users, as it does not collect personal information in general. The app is designed to function without any personal data being transmitted to the developer.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This privacy policy may be updated if the app's features change in a way that affects how information is handled. When updated, the revised policy will be posted on this page with a new effective date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
