Letterseal

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 9, 2026

Last Updated: October 20, 2026 (version 3.0)

Introduction

Letterseal lets you write letters to your future self, or to someone else, that stay sealed until a date you choose. The app works without accounts, servers, tracking, analytics or advertising. It makes no network connections of its own (purchases go through Apple’s App Store), and the developer never receives anything you write, record, film or photograph.

Information We Do Not Collect

The developer does not collect any personal information. Specifically, the developer does not collect:

  • Name, email address, phone number, or physical address
  • Contact list or address book data
  • Your letters, photos, voice recordings, videos or replies
  • Precise or approximate location data
  • Financial account information or payment card information
  • Advertising identifiers or device identifiers used for tracking
  • Analytics, usage or behavioral data

Information Stored Locally on Your Device

Letterseal stores the following only on your device: the letters you write, photos, voice recordings and videos you choose to add, your replies, the writing question you picked, opening dates, the paper, wax colour and initials you chose for a letter, an unfinished draft, the name you sign letters with (if you enter one), letters other people sent you, and app settings. This content lives in the app’s own protected storage and is encrypted at rest by iOS. None of it is transmitted to the developer or to any server. If iCloud Backup or a computer backup is turned on for your iPhone, iOS includes the app’s data in that backup, as it does for every app.

For a “surprise” letter, the app chooses the opening date on your device and keeps it out of every screen, widget and notification until the letter opens. The date is stored only on your device.

A video you add is re-encoded on your device before it is kept; location and other identifying metadata are removed in that step.

Letters You Send to Someone Else

You can seal a letter for another person and hand it over yourself, as a single “.sealedletter” file, using the iOS share sheet (for example AirDrop, Messages, Mail or Files). The file is created on your device and goes only where you send it; the developer never receives it and there is no server in between. When the person opens the file with Letterseal, it is added to their app as a sealed letter that the app does not show before its opening date.

Please know what the file protects and what it does not. The title, the message and every photo, recording or video in it are encrypted, so the letter cannot be read by opening the file in another app, and any change to it is detected. The opening date, the names of the sender and recipient, and the date it was written are readable in the file, because the recipient’s app shows them before the letter opens. The key that opens the letter travels inside the file, so the seal is a promise the app keeps, not a vault: someone determined to take the file apart could read it. Send letters only to people and places you trust.

A letter someone sends you is stored on your device like your own letters. Its sender’s name is shown as written in the file.

Backups You Create

You can export your letters, with their photos, recordings and videos, to a single backup file protected by a password you choose. The file is created on your device and encrypted there (AES-GCM, with a key derived from your password using PBKDF2) before you decide where to save or send it, for example the Files app or AirDrop. The developer never receives the file or the password, and the password is never stored, so nobody — including the developer — can open a backup whose password is lost. Restoring reads the file on your device and only adds letters that are missing; it never changes letters already there.

Widgets, Live Countdowns and Notifications

So that widgets and the Lock Screen live countdown can show what is coming without opening the app, the app shares a small summary with its own widget through a private App Group container on your device: the names, opening dates and wax colours of the next few letters, and the number of sealed and opened letters. Surprise opening dates are never included. This summary stays on your device.

The app sends local reminders the day before a letter opens and on the day itself (surprise letters get only the opening reminder), and, if you turn it on, one yearly reminder to write. These are scheduled on your device; no notification server is involved. Opening reminders respect Focus unless you turn on Openings break through Focus.

Because the Lock Screen can be read by whoever holds your phone, the setting Show names outside the app lets you keep letter names out of widgets, live countdowns and notifications. It is off for new installations. When it is off, the name is not written into the widget summary at all. The title of a letter someone sent you stays hidden until it opens. The contents of a letter never appear outside the app.

App Permissions

Every permission is optional and requested only when you use the feature that needs it:

  • Notifications — to tell you when a letter opens. You may decline; the rest of the app keeps working.
  • Microphone — only when you choose to record a voice message. Recordings are saved inside the app on your device and are never uploaded.
  • Face ID or Touch ID — only if you turn on the app lock. Biometric matching is performed by iOS; the app receives only a yes or no and never has access to your biometric data.

Photos and videos are chosen with the system photo picker, which gives the app only the items you select and does not require access to your photo library. Photos are resized and videos re-encoded, then stored inside the app on your device.

Payments and Purchases

The optional one-time purchase, Letterseal Unlimited, is processed by Apple through the App Store using StoreKit. The developer does not receive, process or store your payment information, and no subscription is offered.

Third-Party Services

The app contains no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs. It uses only Apple platform features — local notifications, widgets, Live Activities, StoreKit, the photo picker, the share sheet, the microphone and Face ID / Touch ID — none of which are used to track or profile you.

Data Sharing

The developer does not sell, trade, rent or share user data with anyone. There is no developer-side database. A keepsake PDF, a backup file or a sealed letter leaves your device only if you choose to share it yourself.

Data Deletion

You can delete a letter at any time inside the app, which also removes its photos, voice recording and video. Deleting the app removes all data stored by it on your device, including letters, photos, recordings, videos, drafts and settings. Backup files you saved elsewhere, and sealed letters you sent, are outside the app and yours (or their recipients’) to delete. Because nothing is stored on a server, there is no account to close and no request you need to send.

Children's Privacy

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children or from anyone, because it does not collect personal information at all.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This policy may be updated if the app’s features change in a way that affects how information is handled. The revised policy will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
