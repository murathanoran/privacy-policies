How to never forget a birthday on iPhone — privately

Forgetting a birthday is rarely about not caring. The date simply arrives on a busy day with nothing to remind you. Here is how to make sure something does — without giving your address book away.

1. Start with what's already on your iPhone

If a contact card has a birthday saved, the Calendar app can show it on a built-in Birthdays calendar. In the Calendar settings, under default alert times, you can choose to be alerted on the day or a little before. It costs nothing and needs no extra app.

Its limits are worth knowing: it only knows people whose contact card has a birthday, it has no anniversaries for couples, and every alert looks the same as a meeting reminder.

2. Put the next birthday where you'll see it

A reminder you dismiss in the morning is easy to forget by the evening. A widget on your Home Screen or Lock Screen keeps the next birthday in view all day — and shows next week's, so there's time to buy a gift or post a card.

3. Decide what the day needs

  • A heads-up. For people you send a gift to, one or two weeks before is when it's still easy.
  • The words. Having a message half-written makes it far more likely you'll send it before lunch instead of at 11 pm.
  • Anniversaries. A couple's anniversary, or your own, deserves the same reminder as a birthday.
  • Remembrance days. The birthday of someone who has died can matter just as much — but needs a quiet reminder, not confetti.

4. Keep your contacts to yourself

Many reminder apps ask for full access to your contacts, and some upload them to sync across devices or to suggest gifts. You don't need that. On iPhone, apps can use Apple's contact picker instead: it runs outside the app and hands over only the people you tap. Before you allow access to your contacts, check whether the app offers to pick people instead.

5. Make a backup you own

Whatever you use, keep a copy of your list somewhere you control — a file in iCloud Drive or on your computer — so a new phone or a deleted app doesn't take the dates with it.

Where Birthdays fits

Birthdays was built for exactly this: people chosen through Apple's picker, a reminder on the morning of each day that repeats every year on its own, widgets for the Home and Lock Screen, wishes and cards ready on the day, anniversaries and quiet remembrance days, and a backup file you keep. No account, no tracking, never a subscription.
