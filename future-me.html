Future Me

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 2, 2026

Last Updated: September 2, 2026

Introduction

Future Me is designed to help you write messages or notes to your future self, to be revealed at a date you choose. This app is designed to work without accounts, tracking, analytics, advertising, or developer-side storage of user-created content.

Information We Do Not Collect

This app does not collect personal information from users. Specifically, the developer does not collect:

  • Name, email address, phone number, or physical address
  • Contact list or address book data
  • Photos or media from your device, unless you explicitly choose a photo for an optional in-app feature
  • Precise or approximate location data, unless explicitly required by a feature you choose to use
  • Financial account information or payment card information
  • Advertising identifiers or device identifiers used for tracking
  • Analytics or behavioral data
  • User-created content stored on developer servers

Information Stored Locally on Your Device

Future Me may store the following data locally on your device: messages or notes you write to yourself, an optional photo attached to a capsule, reveal dates, reminders, unsent drafts, and general app settings. This content is written to the app's own protected storage area and is encrypted at rest by iOS. None of this data is transmitted to the developer or any external server.

Widgets and Live Countdowns

Future Me includes an optional Home Screen widget and an optional Lock Screen live countdown. So that these can draw the countdown without opening the app, the app shares a small summary of your next capsule — its name and its opening date — with its own widget through a private App Group container on your device. This summary stays on your device and is never transmitted anywhere.

Because a Lock Screen widget can be read by anyone holding your phone, the app includes a setting called Show names on widgets. When you turn it off, the capsule's name is not written into that shared summary at all, and widgets display only the countdown. The message inside a capsule is never shared with a widget in either case.

App Permissions

This app is designed to work without requesting access to sensitive device permissions. It asks for permission to send you local notifications so it can tell you when a capsule is ready to open; you may decline, and the rest of the app continues to work. Choosing a photo uses the system photo picker, which does not require the app to have access to your photo library. If a permission is requested for an optional feature, it is used solely for that feature and is not used for tracking, profiling, or any purpose beyond what is needed to deliver the feature you choose to use.

Payments and Purchases

Purchases, if available within this app, are processed by Apple through the App Store using StoreKit. The developer does not receive, process, or store your payment card information. All payment handling is managed entirely by Apple.

Third-Party Services

This app does not integrate third-party analytics services, advertising networks, or tracking SDKs. If the app uses Apple platform features such as local notifications or in-app purchases through StoreKit, these are device-level or App Store platform features provided by Apple and are not used for tracking or profiling users.

Data Sharing

The developer does not sell, trade, rent, or share user data with third parties. There is no developer-side database of user-created content. Your information stays on your device.

Data Deletion

You can delete a capsule at any time from inside the app, which also removes any photo attached to it. Deleting the app from your device removes all locally stored data associated with it, including capsules, photos, drafts, and settings. Because nothing is stored on a server, there is no account to close and no request you need to send to the developer.

Children's Privacy

This app does not knowingly collect personal information from children or from any users, as it does not collect personal information in general. The app is designed to function without any personal data being transmitted to the developer.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This privacy policy may be updated if the app's features change in a way that affects how information is handled. When updated, the revised policy will be posted on this page with a new effective date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

Contact

For privacy-related inquiries regarding this application, please use the contact methods provided through the App Store listing.
