One Breath: Box Breathing

Breathing exercises for iPhone and Apple Watch · Download on the App Store

Calm down in one minute

One Breath is the quickest way to slow down during the day — before a meeting, after a hard call, in a queue. Tap Calm Now and follow the circle, or close your eyes and feel the rhythm through haptics. No choices, no setup, no account.

Calm Now is a fixed one-minute reset: two deep sighs (a double breath in, then one long breath out), followed by four slow breaths that are longer out than in. It is free on iPhone and Apple Watch.

Breathing on your wrist

On Apple Watch, the taps tell you what to do without looking: they come closer together while you breathe in, spread out while you breathe out, and tick once a second while you hold. Lower your wrist and close your eyes — the session keeps guiding you with the screen off. Start in one tap from a watch-face complication.

Breathing techniques in One Breath

  • Box breathing — breathe in for 4 seconds, hold for 4, breathe out for 4, hold for 4. Steady and grounding. Free.
  • 4-7-8 breathing — breathe in for 4, hold for 7, breathe out for 8. A long, slow exhale. Free.
  • Calm — breathe in for 4, out for 6. Simple and gentle. Free.
  • Physiological sigh — a double breath in followed by one long breath out.
  • Coherent breathing — about 5.5 seconds in and 5.5 seconds out, around five and a half breaths a minute.
  • Triangle, Steady, Release, Energize and Deep Box — more rhythms for different moments, plus rhythms you build yourself.

Moments let you pick the situation instead of the technique: Before a Meeting, After a Hard Call, Racing Thoughts, Midday Reset, Before Focus and Quick Lift.

Private by design

No account. No ads. No analytics. No tracking. One Breath never connects to a server run by the developer — your breath stays on your iPhone and Apple Watch. Read the privacy policy or get help and answers.
